## 0. Executive conclusion

The repository does **not** presently prove either of the following:

```text
F55-YES: the Klein cubic (equivalently its V14 twin) is F55-unirational;
F55-NO:  the Klein cubic (equivalently its V14 twin) is not F55-unirational.
```

The exact arithmetic decision object is sound, several structural reductions
are sound, and a substantial number of bounded or restricted constructions
are excluded.  The former negative endgame through the boundary value-form,
Lemma S, and the conserved-eleven campaign is not a proof: after the index
correction and the later `F2/F3` repair, the surviving boundary system is
satisfied by an explicit integral-sloped PL function and by a convex lattice
polytope.  This kills that proposed obstruction rather than killing a
rational point.

No dominant F55-equivariant rational map and no rational point on the generic
F55 twist is known.  No replacement pointlessness obstruction is currently
sealed.

## 1. Exact decision object

Let

```text
E = C(r0,...,r4)/(r0*r1*r2*r3*r4 - 1),
sigma(ri) = r(i+1),
K = E^<sigma> = C(U1,U2,U3,U4).
```

The generic F55 twist is represented by the cyclic trace cubic

```text
Phi(a) = Tr_{E/K}(r2^(-1) * a^2 * sigma(a)) = 0.
```

The following equivalence is the authoritative endpoint:

```text
F55-unirationality
    <=> the generic F55 twist has a K-point
    <=> there exists 0 != a in E with Phi(a)=0.
```

The model and its torsor interpretation are recorded in

```text
goal_runs_after_35fa/H_11_5_TWIST/
goal_runs_after_141f60/H6A_PROJECTIVE_11_ISOGENY/
goal_runs_after_141f60/H6_TRACE_CUBIC_DECISION/
```

On the projective product-one torus the map

```text
[a] |-> [a^2 sigma(a)]
```

is an isogeny of degree 11.  The dual group-ring operator is

```text
B = 5 - 3 sigma + sigma^2 - sigma^3,
```

and the augmentation-lattice Smith form is `(1,1,1,11)`.  The
order-11 class of `r2` is real and load-bearing.  It is **not**, by itself, a
pointlessness certificate: additive cancellation in `a^2 sigma(a)` is the
unresolved layer.

By the odd-order twin transfer in `theory/FIX_IX_v14.md`, the F55 question has
the same answer on the Klein cubic and on the associated V14 threefold.
This transfer does not itself decide that answer.

## 2. Results that survive the audit

### 2.1 The generic twist and torsor reduction

The trace-cubic model, the degree-11 projective isogeny, the Hilbert-90 frame,
and the equivalence between a K-point and a nonzero trace-cubic zero survive.
The boundary audit in H6 is correctly scoped: the dense-torus equivalence is
exact, while boundary pointlessness was never proved.

### 2.2 Index one and prime-to-three closed points

The twist has zero-cycles of relatively prime degrees, including the
C11-fixed degree-5 geometry and the D12/55-line geometry.  Hence its index is
one.  This does **not** imply a rational point.  Promoting the available
prime-to-three closed points to a point is precisely the unresolved cubic
descent step, not a formal consequence of the index computation.

### 2.3 F55 fixed data on V14

The five C11-fixed points, their tangent-weight pattern, and the pentagon of
contained lines are genuine structural data.  In the Weil-six-space model,
the five points are the five pure decomposables indexed by

```text
{4,9}, {1,5}, {3,4}, {3,5}, {1,9}.
```

Two fixed points span a contained line exactly when the corresponding edges
share a vertex, giving a 5-cycle rather than the complete graph.  The
`u0`-free diagonal-curve exclusion and the small-span curve calculations are
valid at their stated scope.  They do not yet classify all C11-stable rational
curves through diagonal pairs, and they do not prove that a hypothetical
source resolution must produce such a diagonal curve.

### 2.4 The carrier condition

The Hodge-theoretic carrier theorem in `theory/FIX_VII_carrier.md` is a valid
necessary condition: a resolution of a dominant equivariant map must acquire
a center whose H1 contains the Weil isotypic component.  The Hessian curve
provides a canonical carrier already present in the source.  Thus the carrier
condition is useful for organizing constructions and bounded windows, but it
is not an all-degree obstruction.

### 2.5 Bounded and restricted searches

The low-degree covariant, sparse trace-cubic, Palatini, Morita, and other
bounded searches remain valid only at their declared cutoffs.  Empty bounded
searches must not be promoted to all-degree statements.

A separate fully commutative reconstruction of the F55 landing equations
adds the following director-grade observations:

```text
d = 6: support system infeasible;
d = 7: one maximal tropical support of size 18 survives, with 32 feasible
       sub-supports; their binomial phase systems are inconsistent modulo 661
       for all five projective twists;
d = 8: support system infeasible;
d = 9: support system infeasible;
d = 10: support system not decided.
```

The `d=6,8,9` statements are exact support-level exclusions.  The `d=7`
statement is a finite-field phase certificate and is not promoted here to a
characteristic-zero geometric emptiness theorem without an exact
cyclotomic-binomial certificate.

### 2.6 Palatini fixed-point shortcut

For F55 the six-dimensional Weil representation splits as `1 + 5`, so the
unique invariant quartic in the adapted coordinates has, modulo 397 and up to
normalization, the term pattern

```text
Q(t,x) = t^4 + 8 t F_Klein(x) + 16 H_sing(x).
```

In particular `Q(1,0,...,0) != 0`: the unique F55-fixed point is off the
Palatini quartic.  Projection from that fixed point therefore does not give a
rationality shortcut.  The invariant hyperplane recovers the singular cyclic
quartic already present in the known F55 birational link.

## 4. What would count as a decision

### Positive

Any one of the following, with the relevant descent bridge checked, is enough:

```text
- an explicit nonzero a in E with Phi(a)=0;
- a rational section of the trace-hyperplane degree-11 torsor;
- a dominant F55-equivariant rational map from a projective linear source to
  the Klein cubic or V14;
- another construction producing a K-point of the authoritative generic twist.
```

### Negative

A negative theorem must exclude every K-point of the trace cubic.  Examples of
acceptable endpoints include:

```text
- one specialization of the generic F55 torsor to a field over which the
  specialized trace cubic is rigorously anisotropic;
- a nonzero unramified or cycle-module invariant evaluated on the generic
  twist and killed by a rational point;
- a global coefficient-realizability obstruction proving that every possible
  Newton/tropical shadow fails to lift to an exact trace-zero polynomial;
- a complete source/target rigidity theorem forcing a forbidden C11-stable
  rational curve or center on V14.
```

Index one, local solubility, a nontrivial Kummer class, bounded covariant
emptiness, and a feasible or infeasible selected fan are not, separately,
negative decisions.
