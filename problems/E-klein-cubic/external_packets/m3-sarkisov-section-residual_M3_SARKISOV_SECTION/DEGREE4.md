# Degree-four verdict

## Exact result

```text
M3-NO-E-SPLIT-QUARTIC
```

Let \(E/K\) be the generic Schur torsor and let \(q\) be the fibration
parameter.  No integral degree-four point on the generic cubic surface can
have residue field contained in \(E(q)\).  The proof is the certified absence
of index-four subgroups of \(\operatorname{PSL}_2(\mathbf F_{11})\).

## Nonresult

This is not an emptiness theorem for the integral quartic locus.  The
section-or-integral-quartic theorem may create a quartic over a new extension,
and no explicit defining quartic algebra was recovered here.

Accordingly this file is not a headline-negative certificate and must not be
used as one.
