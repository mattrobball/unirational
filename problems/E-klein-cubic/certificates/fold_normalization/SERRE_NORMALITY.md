## 1. \(S_2\) — Cohen–Macaulay / complete intersection (**proved**)

**Lemma.** \(P,P_u\) form a regular sequence in \(R[u]=\mathbf Q[A,B,Y,Z,u]\).

*Proof.* \(R[u]\) is a UFD of dimension 5.

1. **Content.** The sealed polynomial \(P\) has integer content 1 (gcd of all
   coefficients is 1; 1 593 terms).  Hence \(P\) has no nonunit common factor in
   \(\mathbf Q[A,B,Y,Z]\) among its \(u\)-coefficients.
2. **Resultant nonzero.** By sealed construction, \(H\mid\operatorname{Res}_u(P,P_u)\) and
   \(H\neq 0\).  Therefore \(\operatorname{Res}_u(P,P_u)\neq 0\) in \(R\).
3. **No common factor.** Let \(d=\gcd(P,P_u)\) in \(R[u]\).  If \(\deg_u d\ge 1\), then
   \(\operatorname{Res}_u(P,P_u)=0\), contradiction.  If \(\deg_u d=0\), then \(d\) divides
   every \(u\)-coefficient of \(P\), so \(d\) is a unit by content 1.  Thus \(d\) is a unit.
4. **Height 2.** No height-1 prime contains both \(P\) and \(P_u\) (such a prime would
   be principal, generated by a common irreducible factor).  By Krull,
   \(\operatorname{height}(P,P_u)\le 2\).  Combined with the previous step,
   \(\operatorname{height}(P,P_u)=2\).
5. **Regular sequence.** In a Cohen–Macaulay ring, \(r\) elements generating an
   ideal of height \(r\) form a regular sequence.  Hence \((P,P_u)\) is a regular
   sequence, and \(R[u]/(P,P_u)\) is a complete intersection of pure dimension 3,
   hence Cohen–Macaulay, hence \(S_k\) for all \(k\).

**Localization.** On the open where the complementary resultant factor \(G\) of
\(\operatorname{Res}_u=H\cdot G\) and the gates \(\Sigma\) are inverted, \(H\) vanishes on
\(V(P,P_u)\), so \(S\) is a localization of an open of this complete intersection.
Localization preserves the Cohen–Macaulay property.  Therefore \(S\) satisfies
**\(S_2\)**.

Certificate: `s2_cm_certificate.json`.

## 2. \(R_1\) — regular in codimension one (**fails**)

### 2.1 Jacobian on the fold

On \(\{P_{uu}\neq 0\}\), the Jacobian of \((P,P_u)\) has last column \((0,P_{uu})^\top\)
along \(V(P,P_u)\).  Rank \(<2\) if and only if
\(P_A=P_B=P_Y=P_Z=0\).  Hence

\[
\operatorname{Sing}_{\mathrm{fold}}\cap\{P_{uu}\neq 0\}
=
V(P,P_u,P_A,P_B,P_Y,P_Z)\cap\{P_{uu}\neq 0\}.
\]

The mult-1 simple branch is cut out by the sealed \(H\) (input, not re-derived).
The singular locus of \(S\) on the simple-fold open is therefore controlled by

\[
I_{\mathrm{sing}}
=
(H,P,P_u,P_A,P_B,P_Y,P_Z)
\quad\text{after inverting }\ell\cdot P_{uu}\cdot(\text{remaining }\Sigma).
\]

### 2.2 Dimension certificate (exact \(\mathbf Q\))

Using msolve over \(\mathbf Q\) (absolute path `/opt/homebrew/bin/msolve`), two
**independent** pairs of affine hyperplanes \(L_1,L_2\) were adjoined to
\(I_{\mathrm{sing}}\) (no gate saturation required for the upper bound):

| Pair | Linear cuts | Result over \(\mathbf Q\) | Artifact |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | \(A+3B+5Y+7Z+11u+13\), \(2A+11B+17Y+19Z+23u+29\) | **0-dimensional, nonempty** (real isolation) | `t2_msolve/Hsing_cut2_nosat_qq.out` |
| B | \(5A-7B+11Y-13Z+17u-19\), \(9A+4B-3Y+8Z-6u+2\) | **0-dimensional, nonempty** | `t2_msolve/Hsing_cut2b_qq.out` |

**Dimension conclusion.** For a closed set \(V\subset\mathbf A^n\), a generic
codimension-2 linear section is:

- empty, if \(\dim V\le 1\);
- 0-dimensional of finite positive degree, if \(\dim V=2\);
- positive-dimensional, if \(\dim V\ge 3\).

Two independent nonempty 0-dimensional sections of \(V(I_{\mathrm{sing}})\) over
\(\mathbf Q\) force

\[
\dim\operatorname{Sing}_S = 2.
\]

(Modular cross-checks at \(p=67,641\) give 0-dim sections of degree 1115–1129,
consistent with a pure dim-2 component; modular data are discovery only and are
not used as characteristic-zero claims.)

### 2.3 Failure of \(R_1\)

\(\dim S=3\) (T1 finiteness + dominance over the irreducible 3-fold \(D=V(H)\)).
With \(\dim\operatorname{Sing}_S=2\),

\[
\operatorname{codim}_S(\operatorname{Sing}_S)=1 < 2.
\]

Hence \(S\) is **not regular in codimension one**: **\(R_1\) fails**.

### 2.4 Contrast: unrestricted fold

Without \(H\), the same 2-cut test on \(V(P,P_u,P_A,\ldots,P_Z)\) over \(\mathbf Q\)
returns **positive-dimensional** (`[1,6,-1,[]]`).  That fill lives on complementary
resultant components, not on the mult-1 branch.  Restricting by sealed \(H\) is
essential and does **not** re-eliminate \(u\).
