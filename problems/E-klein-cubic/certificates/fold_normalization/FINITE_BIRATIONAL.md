## 2. Finiteness

**Lemma 2.1 (monic after inverting \(\ell\)).**  
After inverting \(\ell=\operatorname{lc}_u(P)\), the polynomial \(P\) may be scaled to a monic
degree-6 polynomial \(P^\sharp\in B[\ell^{-1}][u]\).  Hence

\[
B[\ell^{-1}][u]/(P)
\]

is free of rank 6 as a \(B[\ell^{-1}]\)-module, and every quotient — in particular
\(B[\ell^{-1}][u]/(P,P_u)\) — is module-finite over \(B[\ell^{-1}]\).  Localizing further
at the remaining gates of \(\Sigma\) preserves finiteness.  Therefore \(S\) is
module-finite over \(B[\Sigma^{-1}]\).

**Lemma 2.2 (\(\ell\) does not vanish identically on \(D=V(H)\)).**  
\(H\) is irreducible of degree 43 and \(\ell\) is nonzero of total degree \(\le 7\).
Hence \(H\nmid \ell\), so \(V(H)\not\subset V(\ell)\).  On the accepted line the
specialization of \(\ell\) is coprime to \(H_{21}\) (exact \(\gcd\) degree 0; M2 and
Singular witnesses).  Thus \(\ell\in\Sigma\) is a genuine open condition on the
mult-1 branch, not the empty open.

## 3. Generic rank equals one

### 3.1 Rank \(\ge 1\)

By the sealed construction of \(H\), one has

\[
H \;\Bigm|\; \operatorname{Res}_u(P,P_u)
\quad\text{in }R.
\]

Therefore \(\operatorname{Res}_u(P,P_u)=0\) in \(B\), so over the field \(K=\operatorname{Frac}(B)\)
the polynomials \(P,P_u\in K[u]\) have a non-constant common divisor.  In particular

\[
\dim_K\bigl(K[u]/(P,P_u)\bigr) = \deg\gcd(P,P_u) \ge 1.
\]

(The isomorphism \(K[u]/(P,P_u)\simeq K[u]/(\gcd)\) holds because \(K[u]\) is a PID.)

### 3.2 Rank \(\le 1\), via multiplicity one and the accepted line

**Input (sealed, not re-derived).**  
\(H\) is the unique monic-in-\(A\) multiplicity-one factor of \(\operatorname{Res}_u(P,P_u)\)
whose restriction to the accepted line \((A,B,Y,Z)=(1,2,3,s)\) is proportional to
the irreducible degree-21 factor \(H_{21}(s)\) of the line discriminant.  On that
line the exact factorization in \(\mathbf Q[s]\) is

\[
\operatorname{Res}_u\bigl(P(1,2,3,s,u),P_u\bigr)
=
c\cdot Q_2(s)\cdot Q_{11}(s)^2\cdot H_{21}(s)
\]

with \(H_{21}\) irreducible of exponent **one** and \(Q_{11}\) of exponent **two**
(plus a residual degree-2 factor coprime to \(H_{21}\)).  This is the accepted
shape \((11,2)+(21,1)\), refined by the residual quadric factor; the mult-1
simple factor remains \(H_{21}\).

**Subresultant criterion on the line branch.**  
Let \(K_{21}:=\operatorname{Frac}(\mathbf Q[s]/(H_{21}))\).  The subresultant PRS of
\((P,P_u)\) in \(u\) over \(\mathbf Q(s)\), reduced modulo \(H_{21}\), satisfies:

- the degree-0 subresultant (the resultant) is identically zero on \(V(H_{21})\);
- the degree-1 subresultant is **not** identically zero on \(V(H_{21})\).

By the classical principal-subresultant criterion, over \(K_{21}\)

\[
\deg\gcd(P,P_u)=1.
\]

**Independent bivariate degree check (exact \(\mathbf Q\)).**  
In \(\mathbf Q[s,u]\),

\[
\dim \frac{\mathbf Q[s,u]}{(H_{21},P|_{\mathrm{line}},P_u|_{\mathrm{line}})}=0,
\qquad
\deg = 21.
\]

Certified independently by Macaulay2 (`DIM=0`, `DEG=21`) and Singular
(`SING_DIM=0`, `SING_MULT=21`).  Since \([K_{21}:\mathbf Q]=21\), the fibre rank over
\(K_{21}\) is \(21/21=1\).

**Simple-fold gates on the entire line-branch support.**  
The same bivariate computations give

\[
V(H_{21},P,P_u,P_{uu})=\varnothing,
\qquad
V(H_{21},P,P_u,\ell)=\varnothing,
\qquad
\gcd(Q_{11},H_{21})=1
\]

(M2: `DIM_PUU=-1`, `DIM_LC=-1`, `GCD_Q11_H21_DEG=0`; Singular agrees).  Thus on
the whole support of the line mult-1 branch, \(\ell\) and \(P_{uu}\) are units and
the Cramer-squared factor \(Q_{11}\) is absent.

**Multiplicity-one upgrade from the line to the generic point of \(D\).**  
\(H\) is irreducible, so \(\operatorname{Spec} B\) is integral.  Write
\(\operatorname{Res}_u(P,P_u)=H^m\cdot G\) in \(R\) with \(H\nmid G\) and \(m\ge 1\).
Specializing to the accepted line, \(H\big|_{(1,2,3,s)}\propto H_{21}\) with
\(H_{21}\) irreducible, while the line resultant has \(H_{21}\) to exponent
**exactly one**.  Therefore \(m=1\): a higher power would force \(H_{21}^2\) to
divide the line resultant.  (Modular mult-1 shapes on the CRT primes agree, but
the line specialization is the characteristic-zero multiplicity certificate.)

For a degree-6 polynomial in \(u\) over a characteristic-zero field, a
multiplicity-one factor of the \(u\)-resultant/discriminant is the locus of a
**simple double root** (exactly one double root, all other roots simple).  On that
locus \(\deg\gcd(P,P_u)=1\).  Combined with §3.1,

\[
\dim_K(S\otimes_B K)=1.
\]

The line subresultant and bivariate degree computations are the explicit char-0
witness that this mult-1 simple type is realized on a nonempty open of the sealed
branch (and that \(P_{uu}\neq 0\) there), not a mult-2 or empty component.

**Upper semicontinuity form (equivalent).**  
After inverting \(\ell\), \(S\) is finite over the corresponding open of \(\operatorname{Spec} B\).
Fibre length is upper semicontinuous.  The set \(\{\text{length}\ge 2\}\) is closed
and does not contain the line-branch point (residue field \(K_{21}\), length 1),
hence is a proper closed subset of the irreducible \(\operatorname{Spec} B\).  The generic
point therefore has length \(\le 1\), and §3.1 forces equality with 1.

## 4. Equality of fraction fields

Over \(K\), \(\gcd(P,P_u)\) is monic of degree 1 after scaling, say \(u-\alpha\) with
\(\alpha\in K\).  Because \(P_{uu}\) is a unit on the simple-fold open (gate, and
nonvanishing on the line branch), the double root of \(P\) is not triple, and

\[
(P,P_u)=(u-\alpha)
\]

as ideals of \(K[u]\) (reduced).  Therefore

\[
S\otimes_B K \;\simeq\; K,
\]

so the total ring of fractions of the reduced fold algebra equals \(K=\operatorname{Frac}(B)\).
In particular the dominant map \(\operatorname{Spec} S\to\operatorname{Spec} B\) is **birational**.

## 5. Multiplicity-one branch identification

1. \(B=R/(H)\) is built from the sealed mult-1 factor selected by the accepted
   line certificate \(H_{21}\) (unique monic-in-\(A\) mult-1 factor of line degree 21).
2. The squared line factor \(Q_{11}\) is excluded by the Cramer/content gates and
   is coprime to \(H_{21}\).
3. Higher folds (\(P_{uu}=0\)) are excluded by the \(P_{uu}\) gate; on the line branch
   this gate holds on the entire support.
4. Hence \(S\) is precisely the coordinate algebra of the **simple mult-1 fold
   cover** of the sealed target branch \(D=V(H)\), on the simple-fold open.

No other primary component of \((P,P_u)\) contributes after \(\Sigma\)-localization
on \(D\).
