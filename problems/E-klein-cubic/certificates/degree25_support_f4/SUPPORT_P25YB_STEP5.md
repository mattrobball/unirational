### 1.2 Monic \(K^3\) — CLOSED

All 56 pure-\(K^3\) monoms admit monic rewrite rules with tails in
\(S\cdot B\), where \(S=\mathbf F_{89}[q_0,\ldots,q_{36}]\) and
\(B=1\oplus K\oplus\mathrm{Sym}^2K\) (\(|B|=28\)).

**Consequence:** \(R/J_N\) is generated by \(B\) as an \(S\)-module, hence
**finite over \(S\)**. Linear projection \(Z=\mathrm{Proj}(R/J_N)\to\mathbf P(Q)\)
is a candidate finite morphism onto the support of the relative module
\(F/N\) with \(F=S^{28}\).

Tails of the monic rules are **dense** in all mixed blocks (not the historical
“\(Q^2K+Q^3\) only” shape of the quarantined 842-row packet). That is expected
from an incomplete row span.

### 1.3 Mixed \(QK^2\) block — PARTIAL, not a refutation

After monic \(K^3\), all remaining rank lands in \(QK^2\): **690 of 777**.
The 87 non-pivot \(QK^2\) monoms are outside the 746-row rowspan; they are
**not** relations that refuse to reduce — they are simply not forced by the
subsystem. No free rank-28 structure over \(S\) (seed rank \(690>0\)).

Historical complete profile was \(56+777+9=842\). With only 746 rows,
\(56+777=833>746\), so full classical \(QK^2\) normalization is
**dimensionally impossible** from this subsystem alone.

### 1.5 Specialized fibres (discovery)

At 40 independent random \(q_0\in\mathbf F_{89}^{37}\), the specialized

**not** a Nullstellensatz certificate.
