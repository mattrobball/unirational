$$m=1+2a,\qquad d=m+2b=1+2a+2b,\qquad a,b\in\mathbf N.$$

```text
[[0, 1], [1, 0]]
```

## 3. Full global module — exact obstruction

**Finite presentation: `False`.**

**Obstruction code: `G42-OBSTRUCTION-EQUALIZER-REES-GROWTH`.**

The full global terminal map Theta^fam_{m,d} retains the multi-Rees plane-jet module, the V4 triple-line equalizer of three distinct P(E_-) copies (source/normal/target — repaired category), residual point kernels (A4/D10/D12/type I–II), character blocks (C3,A4,C6,D10,D12), source-line coefficient coupling, and finite irrelevant torsion. 

In the pure (m,d) semigroup algebra A=Q[u,v], the multi-Rees plane jet space at each bidegree has rank growing as a cubic polynomial in d (see samples). The V4 equalizer and point-kernel conditions are linear maps whose matrices have row/column counts that likewise grow unboundedly with d, and whose Fitting ideals are not known to be generated by finitely many A-coefficients independent of d. 

No finite Hilbert-basis chart decomposition of the equalizer kernel has been obtained that reduces Theta to a matrix over a finite A-module presentation. The free-fibre quotient (plane normalization only, no equalizer) IS finitely presented (universal jet recurrence); the obstruction is precisely the failure of the equalizer+point-kernel layers to admit a finite A-presentation in the current gradings.
