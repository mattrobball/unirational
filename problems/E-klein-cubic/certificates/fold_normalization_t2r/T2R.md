## T2R.2 — saturated singular ideal

\(P_A=P_B=P_Y=P_Z=0\).  Therefore the singular locus of \(S_G\) is controlled by

\[
I_{\mathrm{sing}}^{\mathrm{open}}
=
(H,P,P_u,P_A,P_B,P_Y,P_Z)
:
(\ell\cdot P_{uu}\cdot\delta\cdot C\cdot G)^\infty
\subset
\mathbf Q[A,B,Y,Z,u].
\]

**Implemented saturation in this packet (partial).**  
Exact sealed generators for \(\delta\) (Cramer minor in the ambient frame) and
for \(G=\operatorname{Res}_u/H\) are **not** installed as portable sparse TSVs in
the T2R tree.  Computations below saturate by the **Jacobian-open product**

\[
g_0=\ell\cdot P_{uu}
\]

(and, as discovery, by historical modular Rabinowitsch gates matching the old
T2 modular files).  Full \(\delta\cdot C\cdot G\) saturation remains a named
bottleneck (see exit).

\[
I_{\mathrm{sing}}=(H,P,P_u,P_A,P_B,P_Y,P_Z),\qquad
I^{(0)}=I_{\mathrm{sing}}:g_0^\infty.
\]

Then \(V(I_{\mathrm{sing}}^{\mathrm{open}})\subseteq V(I^{(0)})\setminus V(\delta\cdot C\cdot G)\).

## T2R.3 — bounds

### Upper bound \(\dim\le 2\) (**proved**, closed and open)

**Theorem (closed unsaturated upper bound).**  
In \(A^5=\operatorname{Spec}\mathbf Q[A,B,Y,Z,u]\), let
\(I=I_{\mathrm{sing}}\).  The exact characteristic-zero msolve runs

- `msolve/Hsing_cut2_nosat_qq.out` (pair A),
- `msolve/Hsing_cut2b_qq.out` (pair B),

each certify that \(I+(L_1,L_2)\) is **zero-dimensional and nonempty** for the
recorded affine linears \(L_1,L_2\).

By the generalized principal ideal theorem in a catenary polynomial ring of
dimension 5: every minimal prime \(\mathfrak p\) over \(I+(L_1,L_2)\) has
height 5, and \(\operatorname{height}(\mathfrak p)\le\operatorname{height}(I)+2\),
hence \(\operatorname{height}(I)\ge 3\), so

\[
\dim V(I_{\mathrm{sing}})\le 2.
\]

**Corollary (open upper bound).**  
For any multiplicative set \(S\) (in particular \(S=\langle g_0\rangle\) or
\(\langle\ell P_{uu}\delta C G\rangle\)),

\[
\dim\bigl(V(I_{\mathrm{sing}})\cap D(S)\bigr)
\le
\dim V(I_{\mathrm{sing}})
\le 2.
\]

This uses **no genericity** of the linears — only the height form of PIT.  It is
precisely the direction that two nonempty 0-dim sections **can** support.  It does
**not** give \(\dim=2\).

### Lower bound \(\dim\ge 2\) on the open (**not proved**)

Acceptable certificates (repair §6) include: height-three prime meeting the open;
finite dominant two-parameter map; Noether normalization with two algebraically
independent coordinates; exact irreducible two-dimensional component plus gate
nonvanishing.

**Not acceptable:** random or hand-selected linear sections alone (repair §1.1).

| Probe | Result | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Exact Q cut2 unsaturated (pairs A,B) | 0-dim nonempty | upper bound only |
| Modular \(p=67,641\) historical sat cut2 | 0-dim, deg 1115 / 1129 | discovery only |
| Modular \(p=67\) sat by \(\ell P_{uu}\) cut2 | 0-dim, deg 1113 | discovery only |
| Exact Q sat by \(\ell P_{uu}\) cut2 | attempted under 8 GiB; see artifacts / STOP | not a lower-bound theorem |
| Height-3 prime / Noether / dominant map / Jac-rank point | **not obtained** | bottleneck |

**Conclusion.** Lower bound \(\dim I_{\mathrm{sing}}^{\mathrm{open}}\ge 2\) on
\(D(G\Sigma)\) is **not** certified in characteristic zero.

### Combined dimension status

\[
\dim\operatorname{Sing}(S_G)
\in
\{-\infty,0,1,2\}
\quad\text{with proved upper bound }\le 2;
\]

nonemptiness on the full gate open and equality with 2 are open.  Therefore
neither \(R_1\) nor its failure is proved on \(S_G\).
