# Systematic genuine-boundary census

## Governing filters

Any negative valuation of the genuine twist must pass all three filters:

1. **Unramified torsor.** Every valuation with nontrivial torsor inertia has
   a local point by the central-inertia theorem.
2. **Positive-dimensional residue.** An unramified residue torsor over `C`
   is trivial, so every closed-residue valuation is locally soluble.
3. **Pointless index-one residue twist.** Universal effective cycles force
   local index one.  An index-three or degree-`3Z` conclusion cannot describe
   the full genuine twist.

These filters apply to divisorial, toroidal, subgroup, and higher-rank
valuations.  Tropical value-group nonintersection is also impossible: in
the ramified case there is an actual local point; in the unramified case the
splitting extension and base have the same value group.

## Residual pointlessness audit

Only the selected fixed plane cubic presently has a complete residual
pointlessness proof: the normal universal net incidence has horizontal
generic degree subgroup generated by `3,0,3`, hence index three.  It fails
the genuine-object filter.

For `Q5` and `Q6`, the honest residual object is the full five-coordinate
genuine residue twist.  Its index is one.  Existing finite-height searches,
constant-coordinate screens, and one noncube line obstruction are bounded or
sectionwise and therefore nonverdicts.  No norm-form, Brauer, cohomological,
good-reduction, or proper-subgroup theorem currently proves it pointless.

For subgroup compactifications, nontrivial inertia is positive rather than
negative.  An unramified positive-dimensional residue subgroup twist could
still be pointless, but no accepted proper-subgroup generic-twist theorem
does so.  This packet does not relabel that open route as a V2 pass.

## Search conclusion

The named compactification, discriminant, toroidal, subgroup,
representation, target, and Pfaffian families yield no new genuine negative
place.  The exact new result is the retirement of the proposed fixed-frame
transfer at `D`: its visible ramification belongs to the disjoint scalar
`mu3` cover, while the index-three fibre belongs to an auxiliary slice.
`Q5` and `Q6` remain the minimal exact known-unramified frontier; the actual
unramified branch at `D` would require its own genuine residue-twist analysis.
