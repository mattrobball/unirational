# Two-transversal repair-family boundary

Vary the two chosen transversals in their `P1` families and take the
hyperplane containing them.  On the affine parameter chart `(a,b)` and the
hyperplane chart `h4 != 0`, the 169 non-identical pairwise line-intersection
conditions reduce over `F_67` to scalar polynomials of bidegree at most
`(2,2)` after removing projective and Pluecker scale factors.

Singular factors all 169 scalars and checks every pairwise intersection of
the 164 irreducible factors over the algebraic closure.  Although degenerate
parameter points can satisfy 27 non-identical edge equations, every point
with at least the required 16 is rejected because marked points collide,
marked vectors vanish, or line components coincide.  Hence this chart has no
19 distinct marked lines with the 18 total graph edges required for a
connected union.

The result is deliberately scoped.  It is in characteristic 67, uses the
affine parameter chart and `h4 != 0`, and fixes one combinatorial 19-triple
cover.  It neither lifts to characteristic zero nor covers the parameter-at-
infinity, `h4=0`, other line covers, or integral marked curves.
