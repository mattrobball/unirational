# Primary-literature scope audit

Checked 2026-08-01 for a theorem supplying minimality-to-positive-
discrepancy or bounded conductor-pullback support.

1. Spicer--Tasin, *Rank one foliations on toroidal varieties*,
   <https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08100>, assumes the rank-one foliation is log
   canonical and constructs an lc ambient divisor.  It neither proves KLS
   log canonicity nor identifies that divisor with `V(h)` or conductor
   pullback support.
2. Cascini--Han--Liu--Meng--Spicer--Svaldi--Xie, *On finite generation and
   boundedness of adjoint foliated structures*,
   <https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.10737>, assumes klt/lc adjoint-foliated
   hypotheses and quantitative lower bounds on total minimal log
   discrepancies and parameters for its boundedness results.  KLS2 does not
   provide those hypotheses.
3. Liu--Meng--Xie, *Minimal model program for algebraically integrable
   foliations on klt varieties*, <https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.01559>, begins
   with an lc foliation on a klt ambient variety and produces birational MMP
   machinery, not a presentation-degree or source-support bound.
4. Han--Jiao--Li--Liu, *Volume of algebraically integrable foliations and
   locally stable families*, <https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.16604>, requires
   canonical/log hypotheses and control of general-leaf volumes.
5. Trushin, *Contracted divisors and Degree-Two Maps*,
   <https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.26390>, treats dominant generically finite
   polynomial self-maps, with special results in field degree two.  A
   rank-four map from affine five-space to a four-dimensional hypersurface
   has relative dimension one and is outside that scope.

The search found no primary theorem that converts least polynomial degree of
a KLS covariant into `A_E>=1` or bounds the reduced preimage of conductor
primes.  This is a current theorem-supply audit, not a claim about future
mathematics.
