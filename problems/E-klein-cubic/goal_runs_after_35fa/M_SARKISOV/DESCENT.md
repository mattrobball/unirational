# Exact descent ledger

## Source torsor

The source is the genuine projective Schur torsor

\[
\operatorname{Spec}E\to\operatorname{Spec}K,
\quad E=\mathbf C(\mathbf P(V_6)),\quad K=E^G.
\]

It is connected and generically free. The equal-degree ratios
\(q_i/I_8\) are degree-zero equivariant rational vectors, hence a basis of
the descended Klein vector space over \(K\). Every displayed coordinate and
map is therefore a \(K\)-object.

## Center and contractions

The ideal \((a_3,a_4)\), the graph equation
\(a_3t-a_4s=0\), and the divisor classes \(H,D,L\) are defined over
\(K\). Ordinary blowup commutes with scalar extension. Both semiample
linear systems \(|H|\) and \(|L|\) are descended, so no contraction is
constructed only after passing to \(E\).

## The degree-55 multisection

For an involution \(t\), the line \(L_t=\mathbf P(E_-(t))\) is contained in
the Klein cubic and has setwise stabilizer \(D_{12}\). The associated
transitive \(G\)-set twists to the connected residue field \(E^{D_{12}}\) of
degree 55. Simultaneous plane-line disjointness is checked on the whole orbit,
so the union of the projected lines and its degree-55 generic point descend
as a single \(K\)-object.

The geometric lines meet one another at special points, so their total union
is not used as a smooth blowup center. Only their normalized horizontal
strict transforms are used as a multisection.

## Rigidity boundary

Split complex \(G\)-birational rigidity does not classify semilinear centers
on this generic twist. A center after untwisting is a parameter-dependent
\(G\)-equivariant map into a Hilbert scheme, not necessarily a constant
\(G\)-fixed center. No negative inference in this packet uses split rigidity.
