## G4.0 — exact induction to the generic G-torsor

For each `A5` class:

1. construct the 11-element coset action `G/H` from the exact group
   generators;
2. build the finite etale algebra `L_H/K_proj` attached to the genuine generic
   `G`-torsor;
3. base-change the installed `A5` point formula to the corresponding
   `H`-reduction;
4. transport all eleven conjugates to the authoritative normalized G3 frame;
5. verify each conjugate satisfies `Phi=0` over `L_H`;
6. prove the resulting cycle is defined over `K_proj`, reduced on an explicit
   open, and has exact degree 11.

generic statement that an `A5` twist is soluble is not an executable induced

## G4.1 — permutation-module decomposition

Compute the exact decomposition of the 11-dimensional permutation module over
the constant field and over the actual descent field.  The expected complex
shape is a trivial line plus two five-dimensional constituents, but the worker
must verify:

- character values and primitive central idempotents;
- which constituent is the Klein representation and which is its companion;
- fields of definition and any Galois interchange;
- exact projectors in the coset basis;
- compatibility with both maximal `A5` classes.

Apply the projectors to the eleven point vectors and to their degree-two and
degree-three moment tensors.  Enumerate the complete low-arity
`W`-isotypic output space, rather than testing a hand-picked trace.

Deliver a small exact basis of every candidate `K_proj`-rational vector or
frame produced from the cycle.

## G4.3 — Galois-stable secant and scroll geometry

1. its linear span and all low-degree equations;
2. Galois-stable secant, trisecant, and tangent loci;

multiplicities and base loci.  Do not repeat a nonfunctorial binary chord tree;
use a construction invariant under the actual 11-point Galois action.
