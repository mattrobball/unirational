## Correction to the marked reflection formula

With a type-I origin, one residual reflection is inversion and the residual
rotation is translation by `q`. Therefore the three reflections are

\[
P\longmapsto iq-P,
\qquad i=0,1,2.
\]

Their products are the order-three translations required by `S_3`. The older
three maps indexed by nonzero two-torsion have products which are nonzero
two-torsion translations and therefore cannot be the three reflections of one
`S_3` action. The old finite verifier did not check this group-law assertion.
