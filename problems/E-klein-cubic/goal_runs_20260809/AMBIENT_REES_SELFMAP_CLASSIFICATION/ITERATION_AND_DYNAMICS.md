# Iteration and dynamics

Ambient landing maps are closed under postcomposition by arbitrary rational
`G`-selfmaps of `X`. In particular, if `A` is one ambient landing map and
`sigma` is the accepted intrinsic selfmap of degree `q>=3`, then

\[
A_m=\sigma^m\circ A
\]

is ambient for every `m` and

\[
\deg(A_m|_X)=q^m\deg(A|_X).
\]

Thus the ambient-extendable restriction set, when nonempty, already has
unbounded dynamical degree in the elementary sense of topological degree.

At the tuple level, if `S` is an ambient lift of `sigma` satisfying
`F(S)=F B`, then the iterated landing tuples are obtained by repeated
substitution into `P`. Common factors may be removed after each substitution
without losing the landing identity.

The resulting base ideals need not have stable Rees type. In fact the work
order's proposed use of iteration as a finiteness obstruction runs in the
opposite direction: iteration proves that a nonempty ambient category cannot
have a finite classification recording degree. Any dynamical Rees theorem must
therefore aim to derive a contradiction from the existence of the whole
postcomposition tower.
