# Adversarial tests

The packet tests the requested rigidity architecture against the following failure modes.

1. **Free-locus Rees valuations.** Not excluded; the landing identity only forces their residue maps into `X`.
2. **Multiple valuations over one elliptic.** Allowed in a two-dimensional transverse regular local ring; no one-fibered theorem applies.
3. **Reducible/non-normal first carrier.** Ordinary blowup is not canonical; normalization can split exceptional data.
4. **Horizontal divisor without horizontal fixed curve.** Not excluded.
5. **Elliptic carrier only after higher blowups.** Explicitly compatible with the parity analysis.
6. **Positive base correction.** Not excluded; polarization formulas retain `B.C` and `B.R`.
7. **Carrier degree >1 over the source fixed curve.** Not excluded.
8. **Nonzero `E[2]` translation.** Symmetry allows it absent the stronger marked-unbroken hypotheses.
9. **High-degree line maps.** Residual symmetry admits them.
10. **V4 bypass curves.** The quadratic product-zero relation sends first exceptional geometry toward the V4 triangle rather than eliminating bypasses.
11. **Finite covers in normalized Rees fibres.** Not excluded.
12. **Singular/nonreduced branch.** Not controlled before Rees centers are known.
13. **Positive-genus hidden centers.** Not excluded.
14. **Invariant linear combinations creating new Rees types.** The landing locus is nonlinear and finite generation of covariants does not prevent this.
15. **Composition creating new base strata.** Expected and harmless: postcomposition is an exact closure operation.
16. **Tangent-residual map accidentally ambient.** If even one such lift exists, the ambient degree-one and finite-profile targets are immediately false.
17. **Torsor-preserving but ambient obstruction in codimension two.** Possible; the defect `A_S` is a global nonlinear lift condition.
18. **Constant strict fixed-network map with dominant exceptional carrier.** Not excluded.
19. **Degree-three ambient survivor.** Not excluded; indeed any ambient map plus a degree-three intrinsic selfmap would generate one at multiplied degree.
20. **Noncanonical Stein model.** Not excluded and not needed for the postcomposition theorem.

## New decisive adversarial test

21. **Postcomposition closure.** Any proposed theorem saying that a nonempty ambient category has only identity, only degree-one maps, or finitely many global degrees is refuted by composing one ambient landing map with the accepted nonidentity intrinsic selfmap and its iterates.

This test is theorem-level and does not depend on a choice of resolution or Rees model.
