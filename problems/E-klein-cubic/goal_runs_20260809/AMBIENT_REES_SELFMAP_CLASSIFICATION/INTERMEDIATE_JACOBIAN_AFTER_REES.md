# Intermediate Jacobian after Rees

The intermediate Jacobian route remains conditional on the actual exceptional
centers. For a resolution of a rational selfmap, blowups of positive-genus
curves contribute additional `H^1` and hence Jacobian summands. Therefore the
uncorrected identity `u^dagger u=[delta]` cannot be assumed.

The normalized-Rees program could revive this route only by proving that every
center relevant to an ambient landing ideal is rational, or by computing the
positive-genus correction exactly. The forced involution plus-planes intersect
`X` in elliptics, so positive-genus geometry is present from the outset and
cannot be discarded formally.

No Rees theorem in this packet removes those corrections. Consequently the CM
structure `J(X) ~= E^5` does not presently give a degree restriction on ambient
landing maps.

Again, any eventual Jacobian argument forcing every ambient restriction to
have degree one would, by postcomposition closure, actually prove that no
ambient landing map exists.
