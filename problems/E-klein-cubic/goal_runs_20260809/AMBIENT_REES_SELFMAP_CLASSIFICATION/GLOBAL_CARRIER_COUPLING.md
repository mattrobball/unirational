# Global carrier coupling

All 55 involution planes and all 55 V4 configurations arise from one global
covariant tuple. Hence normal orders and local initial ideals are conjugate on
G-orbits. At each type-II point the three local plus-plane ideals satisfy the
single intersection constraint

\[
I_P\subset(bc,bd,cd)
\]

and the quadratic landing relation forces one of the three quadratic
coefficients to vanish.

By G-conjugacy the same phenomenon occurs at every type-II point. However the
residual `A4/V4=C3` cycles the three type-II points on the fixed line rather
than fixing one point, so the product-zero equation does not select a globally
preferred missing character at an individual point. The missing direction can
rotate around each local orbit.

Therefore the proved coupling is a synchronized *degeneracy condition*, not a
unique carrier incidence pattern. Higher Rees valuations can restore missing
directions, and no argument currently bounds their order or number.

Moreover a finite global carrier classification including restriction degree
cannot exist if any ambient landing tuple exists, by postcomposition with the
unbounded-degree intrinsic selfmap monoid.
