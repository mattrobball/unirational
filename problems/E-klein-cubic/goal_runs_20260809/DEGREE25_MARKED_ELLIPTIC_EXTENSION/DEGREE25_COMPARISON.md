# Comparison with the installed degree-25 lifting tower

## 1. What the stored tower studies

The degree-25 tower is the `(m,d)=(1,25)` normal expansion along an involution
plus-plane `Z_t=P(W_+(t))`. Its defining state has

\[
p|_{Z_t}=0
\]

and a first nonzero odd normal jet valued in `W_-(t)`. The repaired transition
category distinguishes three different copies of `P(W_-(t))`:

1. the source fixed line in `P(W)`;
2. the exceptional normal-direction factor over the plus-plane;
3. the target fixed line in `X^t`.

The normal-jet tower primarily describes the map from the exceptional normal
geometry to the target line, together with separate terminal source-line
coefficient coupling. A surviving formal state is explicitly not a global
covariant.

## 2. What the canonical boundary map requires

The map $[-5]:E_t\to E_t$ has a nonzero degree-25 coordinate line on the
elliptic and therefore nonzero **ordinary order-zero** restriction along the
plus-plane. It is not a first normal jet and does not map the exceptional
normal factor to the target line.

The exact coefficient ledger at degree 25 separates:

\[
\dim M_{25}=189,
\qquad
\dim(\text{plus-plane arrangement kernel})=59,
\qquad
\dim V_{25}^{strict}=43.
\]

Every state in the installed strict tower lies in the plus-plane kernel. The
canonical elliptic boundary datum lies outside that kernel. No change of
transition basis can identify an order-zero nonzero restriction with an
order-one normal jet.

## 3. Classification of the comparison

The existing surviving formal state is:

```text
not exactly the boundary map;
not a deformation preserving the boundary map;
unrelated to it as a complete state.
```

There may be a terminal source-line coefficient whose projectivization agrees
with the identity on a dense open of a fixed line after multiplication by a
degree-24 scalar. That isolated resemblance does not alter the classification:
the elliptic part, the normal order, and the geometric object carrying the
state are different.

## 4. Why the old survival result does not weaken the obstruction

The free-fiber tower and the later 43-variable global finite tower ask whether
a plus-plane-based normal state can satisfy all coefficients of `F(p)=0`.
They do not contain the canonical order-zero elliptic class. Their survival or
undecided support is therefore neither evidence for nor evidence against an
ambient extension of `lambda_D`. The present obstruction is earlier and
specific: the exact canonical boundary fiber is disjoint from every landing
covariant at order zero.
