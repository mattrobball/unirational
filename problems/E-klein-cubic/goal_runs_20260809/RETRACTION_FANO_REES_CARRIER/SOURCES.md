## Literature

- X. Roulleau, *Genus 2 curve configurations on Fano surfaces*,
  arXiv:1002.4467.  Relevant results: the harmonic-inversion decomposition
  of the incidence divisor, the genus-two component, its residual genus-four
  component, the 55 smooth genus-two curves on the Klein Fano surface, and
  their intersection lattice.
- X. Roulleau, *The Fano surface of the Klein cubic threefold*,
  arXiv:1001.4853.  Relevant results: the Klein Fano surface, its Albanese and
  Neron--Severi geometry, and rank `25`.
- X. Roulleau, *Elliptic curve configurations on Fano surfaces*,
  Manuscripta Math. 129 (2009), 381--399.  Relevant result: elliptic curves on
  a Fano surface correspond to type-I involutions; the Klein action has only
  trace-one type-II involutions and hence no elliptic curves on its Fano
  surface.
- Clemens--Griffiths / the Tangent Bundle Theorem for Fano surfaces: the
  universal family is `P(T_S)`, the cotangent map recovers the cubic, and
  `pi_*(xi^2)=c_1(Omega_S)=K_S`.
