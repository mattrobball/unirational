# Literature audit: normalized blowups and Rees valuations

## 1. Standard results used

The packet uses only the following general facts.

1. **Blowup as relative Proj of the Rees algebra.**  The blowup of an ideal
   sheaf `J` is
   \[
   \operatorname{Proj}_X\bigoplus_{m\ge0}J^m.
   \]
   Reference: Stacks Project, Section 31.33, tag `01OF`, especially Definition
   31.33.1 and Lemmas 31.33.2 and 31.33.5.
2. **Finite normalization over a complex variety.**  The normalization of a
   finite-type scheme over a field is finite.  Reference: Stacks Project,
   Section 33.27, tag `0BXQ`, Lemma 33.27.1 (`0BXR`).
3. **Stein factorization and connected fibers.**  Reference: Stacks Project,
   Section 37.53, tag `03GX`, especially Theorem 37.53.5 and Lemma 37.53.6.
4. **Rees valuations and normalized blowups.**  Standard references are
   C. Huneke and I. Swanson, *Integral Closure of Ideals, Rings, and Modules*,
   and S. S. Abhyankar--W. J. Heinzer, *Rees valuations*, arXiv:1508.05697.
   The exceptional prime divisors of the normalized blowup give the Rees
   valuations.
5. **Graph realization.**  A rational map given by a projective tuple has graph
   closure equal to the blowup of its base ideal; normalization gives the
   finite normal graph used here.  This follows directly from the relative
   Proj construction on projective charts.

No literature theorem is used to classify the special Klein base ideal.

## 2. The joint-residue statement

The center-dimension theorem in this packet is a direct graph calculation:

- the center on the graph closure has function field generated by the residue
  field of the source center and the initial target ratios;
- normalization is finite and preserves dimension;
- a codimension-one center on a normal model has the valuation residue field.

This formulation appears to be standard dicritical/Rees-valuation geometry,
but the exact source-plus-target residue statement is proved in the packet
rather than imported as a named theorem.

## 3. What the literature does not supply

General Rees theory does not determine, for the unknown completed Klein landing
ideal:

- which invariant curves in the first exceptional `P2` are centers of actual
  Rees valuations;
- the irreducible curve components of the normalized point fiber;
- the fixed curves inside faithful stable surface components;
- the residual-`C3` attachment pattern;
- the base correction on a secondary elliptic carrier.

General finiteness of Rees valuations is only mapwise.  It is not a uniform
bound over all degrees or all `G`-stable landing ideals.

## 4. Why normal-cone references are insufficient

The normal cone and associated graded ring control initial equations.  They do
not decide whether a prime survives the finite contraction to the normalized
graph.  The explicit weak-line and weak-conic examples in this packet exhibit
the failure.

Accordingly, literature on equivariant resolution, invariant curves in `P2`,
or connectedness of exceptional fibers cannot replace the missing normalized
local algebra.

## 5. Literature boundary

The remaining proposition is special, explicit, and local:

> Compute the normalized graph of the completed genuine landing tuple at one
> type-I and one type-II point, including its finite target map.

There is no identified off-the-shelf theorem that converts the repository's
formal transition modules into this normalized graph.  The task must use the
actual generators or an additional structural theorem that determines their
integral closures and residue fields.
