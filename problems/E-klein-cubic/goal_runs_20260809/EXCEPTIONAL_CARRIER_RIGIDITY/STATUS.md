## What is proved

This packet replaces arbitrary exceptional sections by intrinsic objects on the
normalized graph of the actual restricted base ideal.

1. The canonical carrier model is
   \[
   \Gamma=\operatorname{Proj}_X\overline{\mathcal R(J)},
   \]
   where `J` is the primitive restriction to `X` of the genuine landing ideal
   `I_f`.  By `AMBIENT_REES_COMPARISON.md`, `Gamma` is canonically the
   normalization of the component dominating `X` inside
   `Proj(overline(Rees(I_f)))`.  Thus it is selected by the actual ambient base
   ideal and is independent of any principalization.
2. For every divisorial valuation `v`, its center `K_v` on `Gamma` has dimension
   equal to the transcendence degree of the **joint residue field** generated by
   the source center and the initial target ratios.  This gives an exact
   survival test for a divisor seen on a higher blowup.
3. At a marked `V4` point, a point-centered divisor survives as an actual Rees
   divisor only if its target initial ratios have transcendence degree two.
   Consequently no point-centered line-valued weak divisor, and no
   point-centered divisor fixed pointwise by an involution, survives on the
   normalized graph.
4. For each original fixed curve `S=E_t` or `L_t`, the ordinary blowup valuation
   has a unique residual-`S3`-stable center `K_S` on `Gamma`.  It has dimension
   one or two, carries a nonconstant actual morphism to `X^t`, and is unique on
   every common equivariant refinement.
5. If `dim K_S=1`, then `K_S→S` is birational.  If `dim K_S=2`, then the
   ordinary valuation is a Rees valuation and `K_S` is birational to
   `P(N_{S/X})`; its map factors through a one-dimensional target by Stein
   factorization.
6. The accepted first nonzero odd normal jet along every involution plus-plane
   therefore integrates to the canonical ordinary carrier over `E_t`, but that
   carrier is **line-valued**.  It is not an elliptic `[-5]` carrier.
7. For a fixed hypothetical map, the intrinsic fixed and stable carrier profile
   is finite and is transported in complete `G`-orbits over the 55 involutions
   and 55 marked `V4` configurations.

## Exact correction to the previous boundary

A prime in a normal cone, or even a divisor created by blowing up a weak-transform
base curve in the first exceptional `P2`, need not be a component of
`Proj(overline(Rees(J)))`.  A line-valued weak divisor centered at the marked
point is contracted to a curve in the normalized graph.  The packet gives two
explicit `V4`-equivariant landing ideals exhibiting this contraction, one based
on the bypass line and one based on the invariant conic.

## What is not proved

The actual completed ideals coming from a hypothetical global landing covariant
have not been written down.  Therefore the packet does not determine:

- all curve components of the normalized type-I or type-II point fibers;
- all fixed slices inside surface-valued point-centered Rees divisors;
- whether a secondary elliptic-target carrier exists;
- the degree `delta` and base correction of such a secondary carrier;
- a uniform finite list of carrier profiles independent of the map.

In particular, this packet proves neither
`EXCEPTIONAL-CARRIER-ALL-PROFILES-IMPOSSIBLE` nor
`EXCEPTIONAL-CARRIER-UNIQUE-PROFILE` nor
`EXCEPTIONAL-CARRIER-FINITE-PROFILE-THEOREM`.

point, prove the following finite statement.

> Enumerate every divisorial valuation above the first exceptional `P2`, retain
> exactly those for which the joint residue field has transcendence degree two,
> and compute every nonconstant irreducible curve component of the normalized
> point fiber together with every involution-fixed curve slice inside the
> retained surface divisors.

The output must include the target component, residual-`C3` phase, and base
multiplicity.  This is a proposition about the actual completed ideal, not its
associated graded ideal.
