# Fixed-network behavior of actual selfmaps

## 1. Accepted strict component classification

Use the corrected August 9 residual action, not the erroneous reflection
formula in the older marked certificate. For an involution \(t\),

\[
X^t=E_t\sqcup L_t.
\]

After choosing a type-I point as origin on \(E_t\), residual \(S_3\) is

\[
\tau(P)=P+q,
\qquad \sigma(P)=-P,
\qquad q\in E_t[3]\setminus\{0\}.
\]

The nonconstant residual-equivariant strict maps are:

\[
E_t\to E_t:
\quad P\mapsto[n]P+a,
\quad n\equiv1\pmod3,
\quad a\in E_t[2],
\]

and, in a standard coordinate on \(L_t\),

\[
L_t\to L_t:
\quad R(z)=zA(z^3),
\quad A(u)A(u^{-1})=1.
\]

There are also genuine residual-equivariant maps \(E_t\to L_t\) of degrees
divisible by three, while no nonconstant map \(L_t\to E_t\) exists.

The reduced network itself has the infinite family

\[
\Phi_{n,m}|_{E_t}=[n],
\qquad
\Phi_{n,m}|_{L_t}=z^m,
\qquad n,m\equiv1\pmod6.
\]

Thus the strict network never implied a finite selfmap list.

## 2. Why the tangent-residual maps evade strict restriction

The new selfmap is constructed from a rational section over the free quotient
\(B=U/G\). The section is not required to extend across the complement
\(X\setminus U\), which contains all positive-dimensional fixed loci.
Therefore the resulting map may be undefined at the generic point of every
\(E_t\) and \(L_t\).

Equivariance only says

\[
\varphi(X^H)\subset X^H
\]

where \(\varphi\) is defined. It does not force the original fixed components
to survive as horizontal components of a graph resolution.

After principalization, any nonconstant fixed-locus map is carried by actual
exceptional components selected by the base ideal. At type-I and type-II
points the first exceptional divisor is \(\mathbf P^2\), with involution-fixed
lines and faithful-\(V_4\) rational curves. Hence the bypasses identified in
the earlier packet are not technical distractions; they are exactly the
geometry capable of supporting intrinsic rational selfmaps.

## 3. Polarization formula

For an actual horizontal elliptic carrier \(C\),

\[
3n^2=3d\,\deg(p|_C)-B\cdot C.
\]

For a rational carrier \(R\),

\[
r=d\,\deg(p|_R)-B\cdot R.
\]

The tangent-residual existence theorem does not compute these terms. It does
show that no universal argument can set every base correction to zero.

## 4. Ambient distinction

For a global ambient landing tuple, every plus-plane is a forced base
component. This is stronger than the intrinsic tangent construction and may
still force a finite carrier profile. The strict \([-5]/\mathrm{id}\) profile
cannot be realized at order zero, but an exceptional realization has not been
excluded.

## 5. Boundary

The fixed-network program cannot classify arbitrary selfmaps: actual
nonidentity maps exist and can place all fixed behavior on exceptional
carriers. Its remaining legitimate target is the fixed-carrier structure of
**ambient landing ideals**.
