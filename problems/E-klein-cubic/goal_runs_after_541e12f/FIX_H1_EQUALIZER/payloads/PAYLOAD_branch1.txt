NOTE on invariant multiples.  If G is an A_4-invariant with ord_{P_i}G = 2k and
  (y,z)-degree-0 part x^{2k}, then G.T has m -> m+2k, r -> r+deg G, e -> e + deg G - 2k,
  and its leading datum is x^{...} times (y^2z^2)^k-shifted Lambda(T).  For G = q = x^2+y^2+z^2
  (deg 2, ord_{P_i} = 0) the datum is UNCHANGED, so (3,8) = q.D_B(yz) and every q^k-translate
  inherit the (3,6) verdict verbatim.  For G = (xyz)^2 the datum is multiplied by y^2z^2 in
