FIX-H1  PAYLOAD 0 -- the equalizer criterion, derived
=====================================================================

Setting.  G = PSL(2,11), W the 5-dim Weil representation, X = V(F) the Klein
cubic, f : P(W) --> X a G-equivariant dominant rational map given by a tuple T
of W-valued forms of degree d with gcd 1 and T(gv) = rho(g)T(v), F(T) = 0.

Fix an involution sigma.  W = W^+ (+) W^-, dim 3 + 2; P_sigma = P(W^+),
L_sigma = P(W^-); C_G(sigma) = D12; the residual S3 = C_G(sigma)/<sigma> acts
on W^+ as triv (+) std and on W^- as std (twisted by sigma's sign; sigma acts
by -1 on W^-).  c_sigma = P(W^{D12}) is the unique S3-fixed point of P_sigma,
F(c_sigma) != 0.  The three V4's K_1,K_2,K_3 containing sigma give the three
triple lines ell_{V,i} = P(W^{K_i}) inside P_sigma; they are CONCURRENT at
c_sigma and are the three mirror lines of the standard S3-plane; the residual
image of K_i in S3 is the transposition tau_i fixing ell_{V,i} pointwise.
(All of this is recomputed exactly in produce_h1_frame.py, part A1/A4.)

Global invariants (Note II sec.1): the multi-order of the germ at a V4 triple
line is (r; m,m,m), one G-invariant pair (m,r) for the whole map.  Theorem H0-1:
m is ODD and ord_{P_sigma} T^- = m < ord_{P_sigma} T^+.


1. THE LEADING PACKAGE AND ITS TWO VANISHING ORDERS
---------------------------------------------------

Write v = w + y, w in W^+, y in W^-.  The leading minus-term is

    T^-  =  sum_{|alpha| = m} c_alpha(w) y^alpha  +  (higher order in y),

i.e. an element

    Phi  in  H^0(P_sigma, O(d-m)) (x) V ,     V := Hom(Sym^m W^-, W^-),

not identically zero.  sigma acts on V by (-1)^m . (-1) = +1 (m odd), and
trivially on P_sigma, so C_G(sigma)-equivariance of T makes Phi
**S3-equivariant**.

Let e' := ord_{ell_V}(Phi)  (the vanishing order of Phi along one -- hence, by
C_G(sigma)-conjugacy, along EVERY -- mirror line).  In the K_1-frame
(a,b | x,y,z) of the V4 packet's normal form (1.1), ell_V = {x=y=z=0},
P_sigma = {y=z=0} = the (a,b,x)-plane, and c_alpha = sum_k c_{alpha,k}(a,b) x^k
with c_{alpha,k} coming from the (x,y,z)-degree (m+k) graded piece T_{m+k}.
Hence

        e' = min{ j : the minus half of T_j has (y,z)-degree m } - m  >=  r - m,

with EQUALITY iff the leading graded piece T_r (the Note-II cell datum)
has plane order exactly m -- which is the case for every classified branch
witness.  Write e := r - m and use e' = e below.

Define the LEADING LINE DATUM

    Lambda  :=  (the (y,z)-degree-m part of T^-) / x^e
             in  H^0(ell_V, O(d-r)) (x) V ,     Lambda != 0 .

For a cell datum T_r this is literally the leading (m,r)-datum of the Note-II
cell element, and its "line degree" is d - r.


2. THE FACTORISATION Phi = D^e Psi
----------------------------------

Because ord_{ell_{V,i}} T = r for ALL THREE V4's through sigma (the multi-order
is a G-invariant and the 55 V4's are one G-class), Phi vanishes to order >= e
along each of the three mirror lines.  The three lines are distinct, so the
three linear forms N_1, N_2, N_3 cutting them are pairwise coprime and

        Phi  =  D^e . Psi ,     D := N_1 N_2 N_3  in  Sym^3(W^+)^* .

D is the unique (up to scalar) cubic form vanishing on the three mirror lines;
S3 permutes the lines, so D is semi-invariant, and its character is SGN
(the 3-cycle rho permutes the lines cyclically and fixes D; a transposition
fixes one line and swaps the other two, and reverses the sign of the fixed
line's form: D -> -D).  In the (p; xi, eta) std-coordinates D = xi^3 - eta^3.
[Verified exactly in verify_h1.py, check V3, from the frame's exact
rho|_{W^+}, tau|_{W^+}.]

Since Phi is S3-invariant and D^e carries sgn^e,

        g . Psi  =  sgn(g)^e . Psi     for all g in S3.                (2.1)


3. THE ORDER-0 EQUALIZER
------------------------

c_sigma is S3-fixed and spans W^{D12}, so D12 acts TRIVIALLY on the fibre of
O(k) at c_sigma.  Evaluating (2.1) at c_sigma:

    Psi(c_sigma)  in  V[sgn^e] := { L in V : rho.L = L, tau.L = sgn(tau)^e L }.

Restricting Phi = D^e Psi to ell_{V,1} and taking the coefficient of
N_1^e = (const . x)^e gives, on ell_V,

    Lambda  =  const . (N_2 N_3)^e |_{ell_V} . Psi|_{ell_V} ,

and (N_2 N_3)|_{ell_V} vanishes to order exactly 2 at c_sigma (the other two
lines meet ell_{V,1} only there, transversally).  Therefore:

>>> THEOREM H1-1 (the S3-equalizer at c_sigma).
>>>   (a)  Lambda vanishes to order >= 2e at c_sigma -- hence, by the residual
>>>        C3 = A4/K_1 which permutes the three D12-points of ell_V, at ALL
>>>        THREE D12-points.  In particular the line degree obeys
>>>              d - r  >=  6e = 6(r-m) ,   i.e.   d  >=  7r - 6m .
>>>   (b)  The order-2e Taylor coefficient lambda_{2e} of Lambda at c_sigma
>>>        satisfies
>>>              lambda_{2e}  in  V[sgn^e] ,
>>>        a ONE-dimensional subspace of V (computed exactly below).
>>>   (c)  The order-k conditions (k >= 1) read
>>>              lambda_{2e+k}  in  Im( ev_{v0} :
>>>                     (Sym^k(std^*) (x) V (x) sgn^e)^{S3} -> V ) ,
>>>        v0 = the tau-fixed direction of std = T_{c_sigma}P_sigma.

Two immediate structural consequences.

* The tau-part of (b) is AUTOMATIC.  tau is the residual image of an
  involution of K_1 itself, and the cell germ is A_4-equivariant, hence
  K_1-equivariant; so tau.Lambda = sgn(tau)^e Lambda holds identically.
  (Machine-verified for every branch member: producers B5 / D.)
  The genuine content of the equalizer is the RHO-part: the residual 3-cycle
  of C_G(sigma) is in NO N_G(V4), so no cell computation sees it.

* No line-degree-0 cell element can be the leading datum of a global map:
  Lambda would be constant and (a) forces it to vanish.  Positive line degree
  is mandatory, and at least 6e.

Order-by-order codimension count (exact, produce_h1_equalizer.py):

    branch (i)  m=3 e=3 :  dim V = 8, dim V^{tau,sgn^e} = 4;
        k = 0 : 3 conditions   k = 1 : 1 condition   k >= 2 : 0 conditions
    branch (ii) m=1 e=6 :  dim V = 4, dim V^{tau,sgn^e} = 2;
        k = 0 : 1 condition    k = 1 : 1 condition   k >= 2 : 0 conditions

So the equalizer is DISCRIMINATING exactly at orders 0 and 1 and becomes
vacuous from order 2 on; the computation below is carried to order 4, i.e.
four orders past the first nontrivial order.

5. BRANCH (i): THE D_B FAMILY,  m = 3, e = 3
--------------------------------------------

V = Hom(Sym^3 W^-, W^-) is 8-dimensional; e = 3 is odd, so the twist is sgn,
and dim V[sgn] = 1, spanned (exactly, over QQ(om,nu)) by

    z^3 E_y  +  (-5+nu)/6 . y^2z E_y  +  (5-nu)/6 . yz^2 E_z
             +  (-7+5nu)/18 . y^3 E_z .

Leading datum of D_B(yz) (the T5 witness, lam = om^2):

    Lambda_0 = om (z^3 + B y^2 z) E_y  +  om^2 (y^3 + B^-1 y z^2) E_z .

At line degree 3.mu the (3,6) D_B family is D_B(f . yz) (Thm N2B-2; the only
degree-2 chi_x form is yz, so X = f . yz is FORCED), and

    Lambda(s,t) = om g1^3 (z^3 + Beff y^2 z) E_y
                + om^2 g2^3 (y^3 + Beff^-1 y z^2) E_z ,
    g1 = Theta f , g2 = Theta^2 f , Beff = B (g2/g1)^2 .

Hence every possible lambda_{2e} lies in the Zariski closure

    S_DB = { (c1 z^3 + d1 y^2z) E_y + (c2 y^3 + d2 yz^2) E_z : d1 d2 = c1 c2 },

a quadric cone in a 4-dimensional coordinate subspace of V.  Machine result:
the equalizer line LIES INSIDE span(S_DB) and ON the quadric, so the order-0
condition does not fail for shape reasons; it pins the shape parameters:

    Beff = (-5+nu)/6 ,     c2/c1 = (-7+5nu)/18  ( = -Beff^2 ).

Eliminating t = g2/g1 from  c2/c1 = om t^3 ,  t^2 = Beff/B  gives the exact
branch constraint  (c2/c1)^2 B^3 = om^2 Beff^3, i.e. B^3 is FORCED:

    B^3 = om^2 (-5-nu)/6 ,     B^3 + B^-3  =  (5 - sqrt 33)/6  =  -0.124093774...

whereas the trace curve demands  B^3 + B^-3 = kp + 2 = (45 + 3 sqrt 33)/16
= 3.889605496...  (and the conjugate root gives km + 2 = 1.735394503...).
NO MATCH -- and none of the four Galois twists (om <-> om^2, nu <-> -nu)
matches either.  The degenerate limits ord(g1) != ord(g2) give
lambda_{2e} = om g1^3 z^3 E_y or om^2 g2^3 y^3 E_z, which are not on the
equalizer line because its generator has BOTH c1 != 0 and c2 != 0.

6. BRANCH (ii): THE PRIMITIVE CHEBYSHEV BRANCH,  m = 1, e = 6
-------------------------------------------------------------

V = End(W^-) is 4-dimensional; e = 6 is even, so the twist is trivial and
V[triv] = the scalars (Schur; W^- is S3-irreducible), 1-dimensional.
tau|_{W^-} = diag(1,-1) forces Lambda DIAGONAL (automatic), and rho|_{W^-} has
nonzero off-diagonal entries, so a diagonal Lambda commutes with rho iff its
two diagonal entries are equal:

        ORDER-0 EQUALIZER (branch ii):    Lambda_yy  =  Lambda_zz ,
        Lambda_yy = [x^6 y] u1' ,  Lambda_zz = [x^6 z] u2' .

The residual-C3 relation psi(T) = lam g(T) gives u1' = lam^-1 u0' o S and
u2' = lam^-2 u0' o S^2 with S : (x,y,z) -> (y,z,x), hence

        Lambda_yy = lam^-1 B8 ,   Lambda_zz = lam^-2 B5 ,
        so the condition is exactly     B5 = lam . B8 .

(B5 = [x y^6] u0', B8 = [x z^6] u0' are FIX-N2C's two plane-order-1
parameters.)  Both readings -- structural and literal expansion of the three
exact witnesses -- agree (producer C).

Exact residuals, reduced modulo each block's ideal:

    lam = 1     :  Lambda_yy - Lambda_zz = -(P1 c om - P1 c + 4 om + 2)/2
    lam = om    :  Lambda_yy - Lambda_zz = 2 om + 1  = dl  (a NONZERO CONSTANT)
    lam = om^2  :  Lambda_yy - Lambda_zz = (om+1) B2 P1 / 2

None reduces to 0 modulo its block ideal, and each is INVERTIBLE modulo it
(explicit Nullstellensatz certificates h with D h = 1 mod I, producer C), so
D vanishes at NONE of the 9 points of any block: 0 of 27.

Closed form for lam = 1:

    B5 = B8  <==>  P1 . c = 2 om
              ==>  (substituting P1 = 2om/c into 27P1^3-24 om kap P1^2+32kap
                    and reducing modulo c^3 = 3c + kap)
                   4 kap^2 + 27 = 0 ,   kap = kp+2 ,
              <==> kap^2 = -27/4 .

That is exactly the degeneration of the SECOND Chebyshev cubic
v^3 - 3v = -27/(4 kap) of FIX-H0's finding D-2; it fails because kap = kp+2 is
real ( = 3.8896054962...).  Reduced modulo 8kp^2-13kp-4 one gets
4 kap^2 + 27 = 45(kp+2)/2 = 87.516123665... != 0.

7. MIXED ASSIGNMENTS AND THE REMAINING PARAMETER FREEDOM
--------------------------------------------------------

* The 55 V4's form ONE G-class and the 55 involutions form ONE G-class, so
  (m,r) is a single global invariant (FIX-H0 sharpening 1) and there is one
  orbit of V4-lines in A/G.
* A G-equivariant section is a Stab-fixed stalk element at one representative,
  Stab_G(ell_V) = N_G(K_1) = A_4; the cell datum is A_4-equivariant BY
  DEFINITION of the Note-II cell (residual scalar lam), so a cell point IS an
  A_4-fixed stalk element.  The other 54 lines carry the G-translates.
  => NO per-V4 freedom: the branch parameter is GLOBAL.  Mixed assignments
  (different B, or different Chebyshev points, at different V4's) are
  impossible.
* Residual freedom after this: the eigenblock lam in mu_3 (3 choices), the
  branch parameter (B among the 6 roots of B^6-(kp+2)B^3+1 = 0 for the D_B
  branch; one of the 9 points per block for the Chebyshev branch), and the
  line-degree datum f (resp. the unclassified positive-line-degree cell).
  All of these are quotiented by the residual A_4/K_1 = C3 action on the
  parameter space, which is the lam = om^2-mechanics: Theta multiplies the
  D_B tuple by om^2 and rotates (x,y,z), sending B -> B and permuting the
  three planes; the three D12-points of ell_V are permuted simultaneously.
  Hence one computation at ONE D12-point of ONE V4 decides all 55 x 3 sites.
* At c_sigma the three mirror lines carry the SAME branch germ, twisted by the
  three transpositions -- and the twist is automatic (sec. 3).  The only
  cross-V4 content is the residual 3-cycle rho, which is exactly the
  order-0 equalizer computed above.


8. WHAT REMAINS OPEN, STATED PRECISELY
--------------------------------------

The two verdicts above are decisions about the CLASSIFIED branches.  The one
gap, common to both and identical in kind to the two odd-row holes, is:

    the (m,r) cells at POSITIVE line degree are not classified anywhere in the
    FIX chain.

For branch (i) this gap is CLOSED for the briefed cell (3,6): the only degree-2
chi_x form is yz, so Theorem N2B-2's X is forced to be f.yz and the leading
datum is pinned to the quadric S_DB at every line degree.  (A hypothetical
(3,6) family at positive line degree that is NOT of D_B type would be a NEW,
unclassified branch, not a gap in this computation.)

For branch (ii) the gap is open, and here is exactly what it asks.  Exact
equivariance of the family T(om s, om^2 t; y,z,x) = g(T(s,t;x,y,z)) gives

    u(s,t) := [x^6 y] u1'(s,t)  =  B8(om s, om^2 t) ,
    v(s,t) := [x^6 z] u2'(s,t)  =  B5(om^2 s, om t) ,

where B5(s,t) = [x y^6] u0'(s,t) and B8(s,t) = [x z^6] u0'(s,t) are the two
plane-order-1 coefficients of the cell as binary forms on ell_V.  Since
(om alpha : om^2 beta) and (om^2 alpha : om beta) are the OTHER TWO D12-points
of ell_V, the order-0 equalizer reads

>>> the leading Taylor coefficient of B8 at the D12-point c_2 equals the
>>> leading Taylor coefficient of B5 at the D12-point c_3
>>> (both after the forced order-12 vanishing of Theorem H1-1(a)).

At line degree 0 (where B5, B8 are constants) this is B5 = lam B8, decided
EMPTY above; at positive line degree it is a condition on the unclassified
(1,7) cell.  Closing it needs exactly the same instrument as the two odd-row
holes: a classification of the (1, odd r) cell at line degree >= 36
(Theorem H1-1(a) forces d - r >= 36 there, i.e. d >= 43).
