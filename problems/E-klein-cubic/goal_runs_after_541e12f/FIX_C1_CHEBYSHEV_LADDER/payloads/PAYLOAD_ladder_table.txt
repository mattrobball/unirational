================================================================================

LEVEL EQUATION (Note II, Def. 3.1), in the (x,y,z)-adic filtration at a
representative V4 triple line, with p0 the branch seed and e_l = T_{r+l}:

    D_{p0}(e_l) := 3 Phi(p0, p0, e_l)  =  - R_l
    R_l = sum_{i+j=l, i,j>=1} 3 Phi(p0,e_i,e_j)
        + sum_{i+j+k=l, i,j,k>=1}   Phi(e_i,e_j,e_k)

e_l ranges over V_{r+l}(m, lam): V4-equivariant (fixed parity pattern per slot),
residual-C3-equivariant with the seed's own lam, ord_{P_i} >= m for i = 1,2,3.
F(p0) = 0 exactly, so R_1 = 0 and level 1 is homogeneous.

"rows" counts psi-orbits of target monomials (the level equation is
psi-invariant; verified exactly).   coker = rows - rank.

D. CONTRAST WITH THE FABLE RECORD

The (3,.) D_B germ IS the closed Fable branch's boundary data ([E15]: the
factorized and nonfactorized families both die at the I^(11)/I^(13) gate).
This packet's ladder does NOT see that death, because it is graded by the
(x,y,z)-adic filtration at ONE V4 line with only A4-equivariance imposed,
whereas Fable's ladder is graded by the symbolic powers I^(m) of the whole
55-plane arrangement.  Calibration conclusion:

    "extends through level N" from this machinery is WEAK evidence;
    only its OBSTRUCTED verdicts are new information.

V4-line piece is T_7 = h(a,b) T_0 with deg h = d-7, and the level-2 equation
becomes h . D_{T_0}(T_9) = -3 Phi(T_0,T_8,T_8): an extra divisibility condition.

UNOBSTRUCTED at line degree 0 does NOT propagate upward.
