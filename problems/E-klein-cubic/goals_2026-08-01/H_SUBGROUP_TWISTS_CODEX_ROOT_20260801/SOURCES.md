# Sources and theorem boundary

1. Alexander Duncan and Zinovy Reichstein, *Versality of algebraic group
   actions and rational points on twisted varieties*, arXiv:1109.6093,
   especially Theorem 1.1.  This supplies the twist/weak-versality and
   twist/unirationality equivalences used in `BRIDGE.md`.
2. Ivan Cheltsov, Yuri Tschinkel, and Zhijia Zhang, *Equivariant
   unirationality of Fano threefolds*, arXiv:2502.19598, Theorem 5.1.  The
   July 18, 2026 author version retains
   exactly the following possible smooth-cubic exceptions satisfying
   Condition (A): `C9:C3` on Fermat, `PSL_2(F_11)` and `11:5` on Klein, and
   the irreducible `A5` pencil generated by the Klein and Segre cubics.
3. The repository's exact group/action sources:
   `certificates/exact_weil_check.py`,
   `certificates/subgroup_orbit_check.py`,
   `certificates/subgroup_secant_check.py`, and
   `certificates/strata/group_subgroups.g`.

The updated CTZ theorem is used positively only for subgroups absent from
its exception list, notably `A4`.  It is not used to decide either maximal
`A5` restriction or `11:5`; those remain unresolved here.

Author manuscript: <https://math.nyu.edu/~tschinke/papers/yuri/25bguni/bguni.pdf>.
Theorem 5.1 is on printed page 20 (PDF page 19); the irreducible `A5` and
`11:5` open cases are reiterated on printed page 23.
