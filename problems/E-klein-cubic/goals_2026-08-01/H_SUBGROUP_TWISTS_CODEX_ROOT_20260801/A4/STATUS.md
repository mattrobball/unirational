# A4

**SOLUBLE FOR EVERY TORSOR.**  Condition (A) is inherited from the full
`PSL_2(F_11)` action.  The restricted action is faithful and therefore
generically free.  Cheltsov--Tschinkel--Zhang, Theorem 5.1, proves
`A4`-unirationality because `A4` is not one of its possible exceptions.
Duncan--Reichstein, Theorem 1.1, then gives a point on every `A4`-twist.
