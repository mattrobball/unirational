# Birational-model ledger

## Original proper model

The genuine generic Schur twist `X_T/K` is a smooth cubic threefold in a
split `P4_K` with geometric and arithmetic Picard group generated by the
hyperplane class.  It has:

- no `K`-rational line;
- no geometrically integral plane conic defined over `K`;
- no nonconstant regular morphism to a lower-dimensional projective base.

The last assertion follows from Picard rank one: a pullback of an ample
divisor from a positive-dimensional lower base would be a positive multiple
of `H`, hence have positive cube, while a divisor pulled back from a base of
dimension below three has cube zero.

These statements eliminate standard starting centers.  They do not classify
all Sarkisov links after base extension or after blowing up zero-dimensional
centers.

## Ten coordinate-line blowups

For every coordinate line `ell_ij` in the exact Schur frame,

\[
D_{ij}=X_T\cap\ell_{ij}=\operatorname{Spec}L_{ij},
\qquad [L_{ij}:K]=3,
\]

is connected finite etale.  Projection from `ell_ij` resolves as

\[
Y_{ij}=\operatorname{Bl}_{D_{ij}}(X_T)\longrightarrow\mathbf P^2_K.
\]

Writing `H` for the hyperplane pullback and `E` for the exceptional divisor,
the exact Picard and fibre ledger is

\[
\operatorname{Pic}(Y_{ij})=\mathbf ZH\oplus\mathbf ZE,
\qquad H\cdot F=E\cdot F=3.
\]

Every generic-fibre divisor has degree divisible by three.  The exceptional
divisor supplies a degree-three point, so the smooth genus-one generic fibre
has exact period and index three and no section.

Scope: a `K`-point of `X_T` can lie on one special fibre.  Hence these ten
models do not prove pointlessness.

## Models after the degree-55 extension

Over `L=E^D12`, `X_T` contains the certified line `P1_L`.  Projection from
that line gives the usual conic-bundle model over `P2_L`.  This model is not
defined by one line over `K`: the 55 conjugate lines form one Galois orbit.
No descended `K`-conic bundle with a section is installed, and no norm or
corestriction operation on its fibres is known to turn odd degree 55 into a
`K`-point.

## Marked degree-55 blowup and curve centers

Blowing up the connected closed point `Z55` gives one prime exceptional
divisor over `K` that splits into 55 exceptional planes over `E`.  The live
degree-19 construction seeks a geometrically integral rational curve through
`Z55`; proper cubic intersection would leave a degree-two residual cycle.

No Mori-cone or two-ray-game calculation proving that every possible link
from `Z55` factors through that construction is known.  In particular, the
following remain unclassified:

- links initiated by other Galois-stable zero-dimensional schemes;
- twisted-cubic, quartic, or higher rational-curve centers;
- del Pezzo or rational-surface fibrations after non-coordinate blowups;
- singular models whose normalization creates new divisor classes.

## Exhaustiveness verdict

The current birational ledger is **not exhaustive**.  It supplies exact
negative results for the original regular model and ten named blowups, plus
an exact positive interface through a degree-19 marked curve.  It does not
meet the goal's requirement for an exhaustive Mori-cone/Galois-action
classification and cannot support a pointlessness headline.
