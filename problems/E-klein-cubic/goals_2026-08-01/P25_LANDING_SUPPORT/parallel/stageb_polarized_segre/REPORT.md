# Faithful polarized Stage-B Segre audit

## Outcome

This route does **not** decide Stage B.  It supplies exact dimension floors and
a bounded low-degree theorem, but no global injectivity, unit saturation, or
decomposable-kernel exclusion.

The polarized map is

```text
L(q) : (B1 tensor Q) direct_sum B2 -> R,
       dimensions 243 -> 690,
```

and its 690 coefficient rows are bilinear forms on
`P(Q) x P(Y) = P^36 x P^242`.  Their common zero scheme is

```text
Segre(P^36 x P^242) intersect P(ker(F)) in P^8990,
```

where `F : Q tensor Y -> R` is the coefficient flattening.  The systematic
`M2` identity minor proves `rank(F)=690`, hence `dim ker(F)=8301`.

The expected intersection dimension is `278+8300-8990=-412`.  This is
consistent with emptiness but is not a theorem about the fixed tensor.  The
largest possible vector subspace of a `37 x 243` tensor space containing no
nonzero decomposable tensor has dimension `(37-1)(243-1)=8712`; the actual
kernel dimension `8301` is below this bound.  Thus dimension theory creates no
obstruction to global injectivity and also does not prove it.

## Exact multigraded Macaulay floor

Let

```text
R_(a,b) = Sym^a(Q^*) tensor Sym^b(Y^*)
J = ideal generated by the 690 polarized equations in bidegree (1,1).
```

Then

```text
dim R_(a,b) = C(a+36,36) C(b+242,242),
mu_(a,b) : F_89^690 tensor R_(a-1,b-1) -> R_(a,b).
```

Surjectivity of one `mu_(a,b)` with `a,b>0` would prove the projective Segre
section empty.  A necessary dimension inequality is

```text
690*a*b >= (a+36)*(b+242).
```

The first possible total degree is `a+b=8`, at exactly:

| bidegree | source dimension | target dimension |
|---|---:|---:|
| `(3,5)` | 72,225,484,767,450 | 67,222,038,141,531 |
| `(4,4)` | 15,267,175,641,900 | 13,607,700,028,650 |
| `(5,3)` | 1,869,450,078,600 | 1,814,360,003,820 |

The smallest target among all dimensionally admissible bidegrees is `(5,3)`.
The polarized coefficient tensor is `91.296%` dense, with `5,663,814`
nonzero coefficients.  A formal sparse `(5,3)` Macaulay build therefore has
`15,345,242,793,443,160` stored entries before elimination.  A direct exact
matrix is not a viable computation.

For comparison:

- the first diagonal possibility is `(4,4)`;
- with q-degree fixed to one, the first possibility is `(1,14)`;
- with y-degree fixed to one, the first possibility is `(20,1)`, recovering
  the degree-20 floor for the q-only dual module test.

The coefficient of `H_R(s,t)(1-st)^690` first becomes nonpositive in total
degree 13, at `(3,10),...,(8,5)`; on the diagonal it remains positive through
degree 6 and becomes negative at degree 7.  These are exact Koszul Euler
characteristics, not Hilbert values: unknown Koszul homology prevents using
them as a rank or emptiness certificate.

## Bounded exact low-degree result

The complete exact `M2` degree-one syzygy computation has rank/nullity

```text
14763 / 10767.
```

The full contraction map from this `10,767`-dimensional kernel to
`S_3^6` has an independently replayed `10,767 x 10,767` nonzero minor over
`F_89`:

```text
minor uint8 SHA-256:
d84ecf7c699f4338e9f2b5cb3a4b17b5052da21837fc134d174fbfc33071abc0
rank: 10767
```

This also proves a new statement about the polarized equations.  Any
q-linear syzygy among their 690 bilinear rows projects to a degree-one left
syzygy `C(q)M2(q)=0`.  If it annihilates all 222 polarized `B1 tensor Q`
columns, substituting the second Q slot equal to q gives
`C(q)M1(q)=0`.  Injectivity of the full contraction map then forces `C=0`.
Therefore:

> The complete polarized equation system has no q-linear syzygy.

Equivalently `mu_(2,1)` is injective, and the quotient has the exact dimension

```text
dim (R/J)_(2,1) = 170829 - 25530 = 145299.
```

Also, the `C(690,2)=237705` elementary Koszul relations imply the rigorous
standard-degree-two lower bound

```text
dim (R/J)_(2,2) >= 14875053.
```

Thus the bounded calculation confirms substantial low-degree survivors; it
does not extrapolate them to a geometric point.

## Theorem boundary

Proved exactly here:

- the Segre/kernel dimensions and the `690` independent polarized equations;
- every raw multigraded Macaulay dimension floor above;
- absence of q-linear syzygies for the full polarized equation system;
- the exact `(2,1)` quotient dimension and the stated degree-two lower bound.

Not proved here:

- surjectivity of any positive-bidegree Macaulay map;
- absence of decomposable tensors in the `8301`-dimensional kernel;
- global injectivity of `L(q)`;
- Stage-B emptiness, Stage-B nonemptiness, or any degree-25 verdict.

The raw Segre-Macaulay route should not be launched.  Any continuation must
replace the trillion-row floor by additional tensor/representation structure,
an explicit finite minor cover, or an equivalent compact annihilator.
