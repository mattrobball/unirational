
## Quartic nonmembership is deliberately downgraded

This packet does not replay the historical `25,530 x 91,390` RREF.  Its largest
dense exact rank input is `4,455 x 2,072` (about 70.4 MiB as modular doubles).

The missing map is

```text
mu : S_1 tensor (V0+W) -> S_4.
```

Until `ker(mu)` is independently decided, rank 2,072 in the formal tensor does
not imply rank 2,072 for

```text
S_1(V0+W) / S_1 V0,
```

and it does not independently prove that every one of the 4,140 quartic tests
or 315 commutator tests lies outside `S_1 V0`.  Therefore the old bulk
nonmembership counts remain strong historical evidence, not a canonical
dependency supplied by this packet.  No pure-`q` annihilator was found or
claimed.
