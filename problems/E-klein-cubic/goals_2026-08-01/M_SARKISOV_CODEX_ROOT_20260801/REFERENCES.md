# Primary references and exact use

1. I. Cheltsov, I. Krylov, S. Ma'u, *G-birationally rigid cubic
   threefolds* (2026), arXiv:2604.20426.  Used only to fence the split
   complex \(G\)-birational-superrigidity result; it is not used as a generic
   twist obstruction.
2. Y. Tschinkel, Z. Zhang, *Stable equivariant birationalities of cubic and
   degree 14 Fano threefolds* (2024), arXiv:2409.08392.  Used for the classical
   cubic/genus-8 Pfaffian context and the warning that stable twisted
   equivariant birationality is weaker than a direct descended link.
3. J. Blanc, S. Lamy, *On birational maps from cubic threefolds* (2014),
   arXiv:1409.7778.  Used
   for the routing census of smooth curve-center numerical types.  The actual
   \((1,3)\) link in this packet is proved directly from the plane pencil.
4. J. Blanc, I. Cheltsov, A. Duncan, Y. Prokhorov, *Finite quasisimple groups
   acting on rationally connected threefolds* (2018), arXiv:1809.09226,
   especially its discussion of the Klein cubic and genus-8 Fano
   \(G\)-models.  Used only for historical split-action context, not for
   descent.

The proof itself also consumes the exact covariant and Weil-pair certificates
from the parent Problem E dossier.  Their hashes are pinned in `SEAL.json` and
checked before replay.
