# Generic-fibre arithmetic and section audit

Put \(K=K_0(\mathbf P^1)\), and let \(S/K\) be the generic cubic-surface
fibre of \(f\). In the explicit fibre equation

\[
 \Phi(a_0,a_1,a_2,su,tu)=0\subset\mathbf P^3_K,
\]

the hyperplane \(u=0\) cuts out the original plane cubic \(C_K\). Intersecting
that plane cubic with any transverse \(K_0\)-defined line produces a
degree-three zero-cycle. Therefore

\[
 \operatorname{ind}(S)\mid 3,
 \qquad \operatorname{ind}(S)\in\{1,3\}.
\]

No degree coprime to three is certified here, so the index is not asserted to
be one. In particular, degree-55 cycles elsewhere on the Klein cubic do not
automatically become horizontal degree-55 cycles on this fibration.

## Sections

A rational section of \(f\) is equivalent to a \(K\)-rational point of \(S\).
Because \(f\) is proper over the smooth curve \(\mathbf P^1\), such a rational
section extends across codimension one. Evaluating it at a \(K_0\)-rational
base point and composing with \(\pi\) gives a \(K_0\)-point of the generic
Klein twist. Hence

\[
 S(K)\ne\varnothing \quad\Longrightarrow\quad X(K_0)\ne\varnothing
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad\text{headline positive}.
\]

The reverse implication is not claimed: one isolated \(K_0\)-point of \(X\)
lies over one member of the pencil and does not itself sweep out a section.

The exceptional divisor is \(C\times\mathbf P^1\). It contains a section
exactly when \(C(K_0)\ne\varnothing\), and a degree-\(m\) closed point of
\(C\) gives only a degree-\(m\) multisection. The intrinsic plane divisor
therefore supplies a degree-three multisection, not a section.

## Rationality boundary

Neither \(S(K)\ne\varnothing\) nor \(K_0\)-rationality of the total space is
proved. The certified result is the descended Mori fibre model and its exact
index bound. Resolving its section problem is a concrete continuation of the
Problem E headline, not part of the structural exit claimed in `STATUS.md`.
