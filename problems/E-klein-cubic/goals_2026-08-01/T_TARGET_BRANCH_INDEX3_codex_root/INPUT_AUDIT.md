## Terminal scope correction

The accepted fixed-frame cubic is a Pfaffian plane curve.  It is not the
genuine generic Klein cubic twist.  Its projector-open points yield auxiliary
Morita projectors, whereas the headline bridge requires a common isotropic
right quaternionic line for all five descended Klein forms.  The authoritative
`FAIL-SCOPE` audit proves these are distinct conditions.  Consequently even a
complete branch-index calculation would stop at `C_fix(K_proj)=empty` and
would not imply `X_gen(K_proj)=empty`.

This correction supersedes the initial exploratory T0 assumption that the
fixed-frame point problem sat inside the genuine twist.  The revised
`T0_BRIDGE_LEDGER.md` is authoritative for this packet.
