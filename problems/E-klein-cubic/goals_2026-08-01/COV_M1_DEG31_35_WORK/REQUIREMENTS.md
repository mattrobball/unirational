# Requirement ledger

## COV2.0

- fixed exact Reynolds circuits spanning all 410 and 637 self-covariants;
- exact Molien equality and independence at two unused good primes;
- fixed characteristic-zero `m=1` bases, not fibrewise RREF bases;
- explicit cyclotomic lattice/open and hashes binding later matrices.

The fixed kernel basis is permitted to be a determinant/Cramer circuit.  Its
dimension must be proved in characteristic zero by both a fixed nonzero minor
and an independent character-theoretic upper bound.

## COV2.1

Install the literal global degree-31 and degree-35 modules through the ordered
plane, triple-line, residual-point, minus-line, `C3`/`C6`, point-link, marked
elliptic, and irrelevant-torsion gates.  A global polynomial source may make
some overlap equalities automatic, but every named map still requires an
explicit proof or payload.

## COV2.2

Handle common scalar factors and known composition images without treating
the linear span of factorable maps as though every vector in that span shared
a factor.  Any quotient must preserve the actual projective landing problem,
not just module indecomposables.

## COV2.3--2.4

Construct the complete landing ideal on the surviving primitive parameter
object.  Decide it exactly.  Any modular survivor must be reconstructed and
substituted in characteristic zero; any emptiness conclusion must cover the
full selected degree and use a valid specialization bridge.

## Deliverables

Fixed bases, all equalizer matrices, primitive payloads, landing ideals,
candidate or emptiness certificates, independent verifiers, status, replay,
and a content-only `SEAL.json` under the contracted destination.
