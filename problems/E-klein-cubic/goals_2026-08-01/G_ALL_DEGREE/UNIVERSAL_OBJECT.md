# The correct all-degree object

## 1. Setup

Work over a characteristic-zero field (k) containing the character values
of the five-dimensional Klein representation (W) of
(G=\operatorname{PSL}_2(\mathbf F_{11})).  Put

\[
 S=\operatorname{Sym}(W^*),\qquad R=S^G,\qquad
 M=(S\otimes W)^G.
\]

The degree-(d) piece (M_d) is exactly the space of homogeneous polynomial
self-covariants (p:W\to W) of degree (d).  Polarization of the Klein
cubic (F\in\operatorname{Sym}^3(W^*)^G) gives the homogeneous cubic law

\[
 q:M\longrightarrow R,\qquad q(p)=F(p),\qquad q(M_d)\subset R_{3d}.
\]

Thus the literal degree-(d) landing scheme is

\[
 L_d=\{[p]\in\mathbf P(M_d):q(p)=0\}.
\]

This definition uses one global coefficient vector.  Every fixed-locus,
normal-cone, line, point, marked-elliptic, and torsion datum in Goal G is a
functorial restriction of this vector; independent choices in local modules
are not points of (L_d).

## 2. Exact symbolic-order filtration

For the 55 involution plus-planes (Z_t\subset\mathbf P(W)), let

\[
 A_m=\bigcap_t I(Z_t)^m\subset S,
 \qquad \mathcal F^mM=(A_m\otimes W)^G.
\]

This is the symbolic intersection filtration required by the work order; it
is not the ordinary power of the reduced union ideal.  For odd (m), the
exact-order source is

\[
 C_{m,d}=\left[(A_m/A_{m+2})_d\otimes W\right]^G.
\]

The map from a global coefficient vector to (C_{m,d}), followed by the
canonical normalization, triple-line equalizer, residual point kernel,
minus-line, (C_3/A_4), and marked-elliptic restriction maps, recovers the
installed transition diagram.  The finite irrelevant module (T_m) is the
difference between the literal graded image and its sheafification; retaining
the original filtered module (\mathcal F^mM) retains that discrepancy
automatically.

Consequently the projective support denoted (\mathcal L_{m,d}) in the work
order is correctly interpreted as the locally closed exact-order stratum

\[
 \bigl(L_d\cap\mathbf P(\mathcal F^mM_d)\bigr)
 \setminus\mathbf P(\mathcal F^{m+2}M_d),
\]

together with its functorial transition labels.  The labels impose necessary
conditions on this stratum; they do not enlarge it.

## 3. A finite noetherian presentation

Let

\[
 A=k[f_3,f_5,f_6,f_8,f_{11}].
\]

The certified Hironaka data give

\[
 R\simeq\bigoplus_{j=1}^{12}A(-s_j),\qquad
 M\simeq\bigoplus_{i=1}^{60}A(-e_i).
\]

Choose these bases.  Expanding (q) in the twelve-element (A)-basis of
(R) gives twelve homogeneous cubic polynomial laws

\[
 Q_1,\ldots,Q_{12}:A^{60}\longrightarrow A.
\]

The affine finite-type scheme

\[
 \mathscr Z_A=V(Q_1,\ldots,Q_{12})\subset\mathbf A_A^{60}
\]

is a correct noetherian universal coefficient object.  Its (A)-points are
precisely (not merely necessarily) the polynomial self-covariants satisfying
(F(p)=0), expressed in the fixed Hironaka basis.  The grading cuts out the
individual (L_d), while the symbolic filtration above records normal order.

This construction includes every global equalizer layer because it begins
with the global covariant module.  It does not assert that the larger module
of independently compatible local states is generated by global vectors.

## 4. All-degree equivalence theorem

Let (K_{\rm aff}=\operatorname{Frac}(R)).  The five primitive covariants

\[
 x,C,D,E,K_7
\]

form a frame over (K_{\rm aff}): the installed exact evaluation gives

\[
 \det[x\ C\ D\ E\ K_7](-2,-2,-2,-2,-1)
 =-295136920\ne0.
\]

Write (B=[x\ C\ D\ E\ K_7]) and

\[
 \Phi(a_0,\ldots,a_4)=F(Ba)\in
 K_{\rm aff}[a_0,\ldots,a_4].
\]

Then the following are equivalent.

1. (L_d\ne\varnothing) for some (d>0).
2. There is a nonzero polynomial (G)-self-covariant landing in the affine
   Klein cone.
3. (V(\Phi)(K_{\rm aff})\ne\varnothing).
4. The generic projective-torsor twist (C_{\rm gen}) has a
   (K_{\rm proj}=k(\mathbf P(W))^G)-point.

Proof:

* (1) and (2) are the definition of (L_d).
* A polynomial covariant becomes a vector in (M\otimes_RK_{\rm aff}), and
  the frame writes it as (Ba), proving (2) implies (3).
* Conversely a (K_{\rm aff})-point has invariant rational frame
  coordinates.  Clearing a common invariant denominator gives a nonzero
  polynomial covariant.  Decompose it by source degree.  The top homogeneous
  component is nonzero (frame independence) and still satisfies (F(p)=0),
  because it is the top homogeneous component of the cubic identity.  This
  proves (3) implies (1).
* The affine invariant field is a one-variable purely transcendental
  extension of the projective invariant field after the certified weight-one
  normalization.  Properness of the cubic identifies existence before and
  after that extension, proving (3) equivalent to (4).

This theorem is the valid all-degree reduction.  It replaces an infinite
degree ladder by one rational-point question, without pretending that
Noetherianity supplies a height bound for its rational coordinates.

## 5. Interaction with the transition machine

If (p) exists, its least odd symbolic plane order (m) puts its initial
class in (C_{m,d}), and every installed transition/equalizer condition is
satisfied by restriction.  Conversely a point of the linear inverse limit,
or even a compatible formal solution of finitely many nonlinear normal
orders, does not define a point of (V(\Phi)(K_{\rm aff})) until one global
coefficient vector is algebraized.

Therefore:

* the transition machine is a sound source of necessary equations and of
  possible constructive jets;
* finite generation of its local/free modules is neither necessary nor
  sufficient for the all-degree decision;
* the only decisive negative output is pointlessness of the generic cubic,
  equivalently emptiness of every (L_d);
* the only decisive positive output is one exact generic point, equivalently
  one homogeneous global landing covariant.
