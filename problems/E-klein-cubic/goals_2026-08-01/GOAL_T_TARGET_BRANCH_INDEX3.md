The preferred terminal theorem is the already isolated branch-index statement

\[
\operatorname{ind}(C_{k(D)})=3

for the multiplicity-one target branch \(D\), together with a fully checked application of the repository's `BR-T-NEG` implication chain. A direct proof that the full fixed-frame cubic has no \(K_{\rm proj}\)-point is equally acceptable.

Do not return a local obstruction, a special-fibre observation, normality of an auxiliary ring, or a bounded multiplier failure as a headline theorem. The final output must contain the complete bridge from the exact algebraic result to non-\(G\)-unirationality.

   \(E=C(A,B,Y,Z)P(A,B,Y,Z,u)\), with \(P\) a primitive sextic in \(u\).

6. `msolve` has falsely reported characteristic-zero emptiness on these systems. Macaulay2 over `QQ`, direct ideal membership, finite algebras, or independently verified exact alternatives are required.

## 2. Exact theorem boundary

A successful result must prove one of the following, with all hypotheses checked on one common open.

### T-N1 — branch index theorem

Construct the normalized multiplicity-one branch \(\widetilde D\), its dominant cubic incidence \(T_{\widetilde D}\), and prove that every horizontal divisor on the generic cubic has degree divisible by three. Equivalently, prove

\[
\operatorname{ind}(C_{k(D)})=3.
\]

Then verify the residue-degree-one and valuative steps that propagate this obstruction to the full \(K_{\rm proj}\)-point problem and invoke `BR-T-NEG`.

### T-N2 — direct pointlessness theorem

Prove directly

\[
C(K_{\rm proj})=\varnothing
\]

for the genuine fixed-frame/generic Klein twist, not for an auxiliary Pfaffian cubic, a coordinate ternary section, or a fixed binary direction. Then invoke the accepted generic-twist bridge.

A proof that the fold is nonnormal is only an intermediate result. A proof that `Pic` is generated by two hyperplanes is already known and is not sufficient: the issue is non-Cartier Weil classes, especially three-primary classes.

## 3. Work packages

### T0 — re-audit the headline bridge

Before computation, write a one-page implication ledger identifying:

- the exact source and target fields;
- the relation between \(F=\mathbf C(A,B,Y,Z)\), \(K_{\rm proj}\), and the multiplicity-one branch field;
- the residue-degree-one statement already proved;
- the precise theorem converting branch index three or \(K_{\rm proj}\)-pointlessness to non-\(G\)-unirationality;
- every place where normalization, properness, or a valuative criterion is used.

If any arrow is unavailable at the stated generality, repair it first or return `T-BRIDGE-BLOCKED` with a precise counterexample/gap.

### T1 — obtain a genuine generic singular/normalization model

Choose one of the following routes.

1. **Full finite algebra over `Q(A,u)`.** Work with the complete gate-saturated ideal
   \[
   I_{\rm sing}=(P,P_u,P_A,P_B,P_Y,P_Z)
   \]
   in \((B,Y,Z)\), not the dead three-equation chart. Construct a finite algebra or triangular/RUR presentation of the component specializing to the certified simple point. Prove all gates are units by exact norms/inverses.
2. **A different valid local chart.** Find a different coordinate pair and a different triple of singular generators whose Delta-open zero scheme equals the full singular scheme on the selected component. Prove the localized ideal equality exactly.
3. **Bypass the fold.** Normalize the target branch directly from the exact primitive equation and conductor data, without asserting an unproved relation between the fold and the normalization.

A finite list of special fibres, interpolation without a uniqueness theorem, or modular dimension counts does not complete T1.

### T2 — normalization, conductor, and local class groups

For the selected dominant component:

1. construct the normalization and conductor exactly;
2. classify codimension-one and codimension-two singularities on the cubic incidence;
3. determine the local class group at every singularity meeting the generic horizontal cubic;
4. isolate contacts locally of the form `xy = pi^m` or their actual replacements;
5. prove that no contact with \(3\mid m\), no multiple residual component, and no other singularity contributes a horizontal three-primary Weil class — or exhibit such a class and thereby refute this route.

Finite jets showing `ord(h)>=5` are not an all-orders local classification. If a formal critical surface is claimed, certify the required localized ideal memberships exactly.

### T3 — global `Cl/Pic mod 3`

Assemble the local data into a global statement on the normalized dominant incidence. Prove, with vertical classes explicitly quotiented out, that

\[
\bigl(\operatorname{Cl}(T_{\widetilde D})/\operatorname{Pic}(T_{\widetilde D})\bigr)[3]=0
\]

in the horizontal sector, or prove directly that the horizontal divisor-degree image is \(3\mathbf Z\).

Do not substitute normality for local factoriality, rational Picard rank for the Weil class group, or a smooth slice for the global incidence.

### T4 — headline conclusion

Apply the bridge audited in T0. State the final theorem in geometric language and separately state the essential-dimension consequence. Include a proof that the object shown pointless is the genuine generic twist required by the source-exhaustiveness theorem.
