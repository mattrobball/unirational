# Primary references and exact use

1. J. Blanc and S. Lamy, *On birational maps from cubic threefolds*,
   arXiv:1409.7778. Used for the ten-type weak-Fano curve-center routing
   census; the selected plane-cubic link is also proved directly here.
2. C. Voisin, *Rank 2 vector bundles and degrees of points of del Pezzo
   surfaces*, arXiv:2509.17996v2, Theorem 1.4 and the surrounding cubic-surface
   statements. Used only for the rational-point-or-degree-four alternative.
3. I. Cheltsov, I. Krylov, and S. Ma'u, *G-birationally rigid cubic
   threefolds*, arXiv:2604.20426. Used to record the split-action rigidity
   boundary; it is not promoted to generic-twist rigidity.

The projective Schur frame and subgroup-line facts are consumed from the
repository's exact Reynolds and cyclotomic certificates and pinned in
`SEAL.json`.
