# Universal coefficient object and exact fibre

Let `S=Sym(W*)`, `R=S^G`, and `M=(S tensor W)^G`.  For each degree `d`,
the coefficientwise Klein identity defines the projective scheme

\[
 Z_d=\{[p]\in \mathbf P(M_d):F(p)=0\}.
\]

For the 55 involution plus-plane ideals `P_t`, put

\[
 I^{(m)}=\bigcap_t P_t^m,
 \qquad \mathcal F^mM=(I^{(m)}\otimes W)^G.
\]

The literal Goal G support in bidegree `(m,d)` is the exact-order stratum of
`Z_d` in

\[
 \mathcal F^mM_d/\mathcal F^{m+2}M_d.
\]

All plane jets, triple-line equalizers, point residuals, marked elliptic
data, and irrelevant torsion are restrictions of this one global coefficient
vector.  A tuple of independently chosen local sections is not a point of
the universal object.

The invariant and covariant modules have the installed Hironaka form over
`A=k[f3,f5,f6,f8,f11]`:

\[
 \operatorname{rank}_A R=12,\qquad \operatorname{rank}_A M=60.
\]

Expanding `F(p)` in these bases gives twelve cubic equations in sixty
covariant coordinates over `A`.  This is a correct finite-type coefficient
scheme.  It is not an effective all-degree decision: an `A`-section may
have coordinates of arbitrarily large weighted degree.

On the open set where the certified frame `B=[x,C,D,E,K]` is invertible,

\[
 M\otimes_R K_{\rm proj}=K_{\rm proj}^5,
 \qquad K_{\rm proj}=\operatorname{Frac}_{0}(R),
\]

and a landing covariant is equivalent, after clearing invariant
denominators and homogenizing weights, to a `K_proj`-point of the generic
twisted Klein cubic

\[
 \Phi(a)=F(Ba)=0.
\]

Conversely, any such point clears to one nonzero homogeneous equivariant
landing covariant.  This reduction is exact and all-degree, but existence of
the rational point remains undecided.
