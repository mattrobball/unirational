# Index and valuation ledger

The machine-readable ledger is `index_valuation.json`.

## Exact index-one theorem

Every smooth cubic threefold over an infinite field has a degree-three
zero-cycle from a proper linear section.  Additional subgroup orbits give
degrees coprime to three:

- for either (A_5), a (C_3)-eigenplane is a projective line and meets
  (X); the orbit of a geometric intersection point has degree (20,10,5),
  or (1), according to its stabilizer, always prime to three;
- for (A_4), the same construction has orbit degree four (or a rational
  point, which is even stronger); the exact fixed-line census rules out the
  latter in the constant fibre;
- for (11{:}5), the five (C_{11})-eigenlines in (W) form an orbit and
  lie on (X), giving degree five;
- for (D_{10}) and (D_{12}), the contained stable line gives a rational
  point after every twist.

Thus the index divides both three and an integer prime to three, hence is
exactly one for every selected generic twist.

This closes H-C negatively as an obstruction route.  It does **not** turn a
degree-one formal zero-cycle into an effective degree-one point.

## Valuations

No divisorial valuation/index-three obstruction is certified here.  Index
one does not logically preclude a local or unramified obstruction to a
rational point, so H-A remains a possible future method.  The packet makes
no claim from a special reduction and no claim that all valuations have been
enumerated.
