# D12 soluble

For an involution (t), its two-dimensional minus-eigenspace is preserved by
(C_G(t)=D_{12}), and its projective line is identically contained in the
Klein cubic.  Twisting gives an ordinary projective line inside every
`D12`-twist, hence a rational point.  `verify.py` reconstructs the eigenspace
over `Q(zeta_11)` and checks all four binary cubic coefficients vanish.
