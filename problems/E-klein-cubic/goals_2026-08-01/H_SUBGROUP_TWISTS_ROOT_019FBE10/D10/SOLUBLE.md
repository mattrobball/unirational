# D10 soluble

The inverse pair of nontrivial `C5` eigenlines spans a two-dimensional
`D10`-subrepresentation, and its projective line is identically contained in
the Klein cubic.  For every `D10`-torsor, twisting this inclusion produces an
ordinary projective line inside the twisted cubic.  Hence every such twist
has a rational point.  `verify.py` rechecks the exact binary cubic identity.
