# Picard group, intersections, and the two-ray game

Let \(\pi:Y=\operatorname{Bl}_C(X)\to X\), where
\((g(C),\deg C)=(1,3)\).  Put \(H=\pi^*\mathcal O_X(1)\), denote the
exceptional divisor by \(E\), and put \(L=H-E\).  The pencil gives
\(L=f^*\mathcal O_{\mathbf P^1}(1)\).

## Intersection ledger

\[
 H^3=3,\quad H^2E=0,\quad HE^2=-3,\quad E^3=-6.
\]

Consequently

\[
 K_Y=-2H+E,\qquad -K_Y=2H-E=H+L,
 \qquad (-K_Y)^3=12,
\]

and

\[
 L^2H=L^2E=L^3=0.
\]

## Curve rays

Over an algebraic closure choose:

- \(q\), a fibre of \(E=\mathbf P_C(N_{C/X})\to C\);
- \(\ell\), a line on a smooth cubic-surface fibre of \(f\).

| curve | \(H\) | \(E\) | \(L=H-E\) | \(-K_Y\) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| \(q\) | 0 | -1 | 1 | 1 |
| \(\ell\) | 1 | 1 | 0 | 1 |

The contractions \(\pi\) and \(f\) give two distinct boundary rays in the
rank-two Neron--Severi space, hence

\[
 \overline{NE}(Y)=\mathbf R_{\ge0}[q]+\mathbf R_{\ge0}[\ell],
 \qquad
 \operatorname{Nef}(Y)=\mathbf R_{\ge0}[H]+\mathbf R_{\ge0}[L].
\]

There is no flop.  The product map \((\pi,f):Y\hookrightarrow
X\times\mathbf P^1\) is the graph embedding, so
\(-K_Y=H+L\) is the restriction of an ample product class and is ample.
The second ray is therefore a Mori contraction.  Its relative Picard rank is
one, and \(-K_Y\) restricts to the anticanonical hyperplane class on each
smooth cubic-surface fibre.

## Effective and movable cones

The ideal of \(C\) in \(X\) is generated by two linear forms.  A strict
transform of degree \(a\) and multiplicity \(b\ge0\) along \(C\) has class
\(aH-bE\), and effectiveness forces \(a\ge b\).  Separating fixed
exceptional components gives

\[
 \operatorname{Eff}(Y)=\mathbf R_{\ge0}[E]+\mathbf R_{\ge0}[L].
\]

Both \(H\) and \(L\) are basepoint free.  Every class
\(aH-bE\) with \(0\le b\le a\) is a nonnegative combination of \(H\) and
\(L\), while positive exceptional coefficient is fixed.  Hence

\[
 \operatorname{Mov}(Y)=\operatorname{Nef}(Y)
 =\mathbf R_{\ge0}[H]+\mathbf R_{\ge0}[L].
\]

All classes and contractions descend over \(K_0\) because the frame, plane,
center, and pencil do.
