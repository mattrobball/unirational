# Primary references and exact use

1. I. Cheltsov, I. Krylov, S. Ma'u, *G-birationally rigid cubic
   threefolds* (2026), arXiv:2604.20426.  Used only to fence the split
   complex \(G\)-birational-superrigidity result; it is not used as a generic
   twist obstruction.
2. Y. Tschinkel, Z. Zhang, *Stable equivariant birationalities of cubic and
   degree 14 Fano threefolds* (2024), arXiv:2409.08392.  Used for the
   cubic/genus-8 Pfaffian context and the warning that stable twisted
   birationality is weaker than a direct descended link.
3. J. Blanc, S. Lamy, *On birational maps from cubic threefolds* (2014),
   arXiv:1409.7778.  Used for the standard smooth weak-Fano curve-center
   census.  The plane-cubic link itself is proved directly here.
4. J. Blanc, I. Cheltsov, A. Duncan, Y. Prokhorov, *Finite quasisimple groups
   acting on rationally connected threefolds* (2018), arXiv:1809.09226.
   Used only for split-action context, not for generic descent.
5. C. Voisin, *Rank 2 vector bundles and degrees of points of del Pezzo
   surfaces*, J. Reine Angew. Math. 836 (2026), 303--327;
   arXiv:2509.17996v2.  Applied once: a cubic surface in characteristic zero
   with a point over an extension of degree prime to three either has a
   rational point or a point over an extension of degree four.

The proof also consumes the exact covariant and involution-minus-line
certificates from the parent Problem E dossier.  Their hashes are pinned in
`SEAL.json` and replayed by `verify.py`.
