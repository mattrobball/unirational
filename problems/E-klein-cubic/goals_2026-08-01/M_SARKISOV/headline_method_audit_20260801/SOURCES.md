# Primary-source boundary

1. Claire Voisin, *Rank 2 vector bundles and degrees of points of del Pezzo
   surfaces*, arXiv:2509.17996v2, Theorem 1.4 and Remark 1.6.  Used for the
   exact rational-point-or-degree-four frontier for smooth cubic surfaces in
   characteristic zero: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.17996>.
2. Carlos Rivera and Bianca Viray, *Persistence of the Brauer--Manin
   obstruction on cubic surfaces*, arXiv:2111.03546, Introduction.  Used only
   to identify the general prime-to-three point implication as the
   Cassels--Swinnerton-Dyer conjecture, not as a proved descent theorem:
   <https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.03546>.

All group, line-orbit, `xCD`, no-line, and fibration claims are consumed from
the current Problem E repository and are hash-pinned by `SEAL.json`.
