# External mathematical cross-checks

The replayable certificate in this directory is self-contained relative to
the pinned upstream representation and twist payload.  The following primary
sources independently agree with its positive `A4` conclusion:

- Alexander Duncan, *Equivariant unirationality of del Pezzo surfaces of
  degree 3 and 4*, [arXiv:1410.8434](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1410.8434).
  Lemma 7.3 treats the `A4` cubic-surface case, exhibits the
  `1' + 1'' + 3` normal form, and uses an invariant six-line configuration to
  obtain an `A4`-equivariant birational model of `P2`.
- Ivan Cheltsov, Yuri Tschinkel, and Zhijia Zhang, *Equivariant
  unirationality of Fano threefolds*,
  [author manuscript](https://math.nyu.edu/~tschinke/papers/yuri/25bguni/bguni.pdf).
  Theorem 5.1 lists the possible exceptional smooth-cubic-threefold actions;
  `A4` is not among them.

These are corroboration only.  The H2 exit is certified by exact covariance,
landing, frame, and fixed-field computations in this packet rather than by an
appeal to classification.
