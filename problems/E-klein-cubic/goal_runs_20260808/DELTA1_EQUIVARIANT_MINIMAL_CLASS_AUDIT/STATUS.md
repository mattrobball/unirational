# Status

`AUDITED / PROPOSED ORBIT-LATTICE OBSTRUCTION NOT VALIDATED`

A hypothetical rational \(G\)-retraction does force a
Kresch--Tschinkel \(G\)-equivariant integral decomposition of the diagonal:
twisting the retraction makes every torsor twist retract rational.

The cited results do not carry that equivariant diagonal to a primitive
integral fixed lift of \(\theta^4/4!\) in \(CH_1(J(X))^G\).  The ordinary
minimal class is already algebraic.  Its invariant Hermitian lattice is the
primitive rank-one lattice \(\mathbf ZM^{-1}\), while integral averaging only
forces a fixed lift of \(660M^{-1}\).  The missing primitive lift is measured
in \(H^1(G,CH_1(J(X))_{\rm hom})\), outside the finite Hermitian lattice.

Elliptic subgroup curves span all integral Hermitian cohomology classes, but
their full orbit sums are not proved to span the invariant Chow group or the
arbitrary Abel--Jacobi image curves occurring in Voisin's proof.  Therefore
an elliptic-orbit saturation/gcd is not a theorem-forced target and was not
run.
