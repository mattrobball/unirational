# Sources and local inputs

## Primary literature

1. Yu. Tschinkel and Zh. Zhang, *Stable equivariant birationalities of
   cubic and degree 14 Fano threefolds*,
   [arXiv:2409.08392](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.08392), Proposition 4.1
   and Remark 3.4. The exact local bridge is also recorded in
   theory/FIX_IX_v14.md, Section 2, and
   goal_runs_20260808/SCHUR_V14/SOURCES.md.

2. A. Iliev and L. Manivel, *Prime Fano threefolds and integrable systems*,
   [arXiv:math/0504595](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0504595), especially
   Proposition conics-5: the Fano surface of conics on a genus-eight
   \(V_{14}\) is isomorphic to the Fano surface of lines on the orthogonal
   cubic. The proof gives the canonical forward incidence used here.

3. B. Lehmann and S. Tanimoto, *Geometric Manin's Conjecture and rational
   curves*, [arXiv:1808.05590](https://arxiv.org/abs/1808.05590), especially
   Theorem 1.1 and the Movable Bend and Break lemma. Both explicitly
   assume that the Fano threefold is general in moduli. Their induction
   exhibits the geometric boundary
   \(M_2^{(1)}\times_X M_{d-2}^{(1)}\), but contains no arithmetic descent
   theorem for a \(K\)-point of that boundary.

4. A. Iliev and D. Markushevich, *The Abel--Jacobi map for a cubic
   threefold and periods of Fano threefolds of degree 14*,
   [arXiv:math/9910058](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/9910058). This supplies
   the intermediate-Jacobian comparison and the conic/Fano-surface context.
