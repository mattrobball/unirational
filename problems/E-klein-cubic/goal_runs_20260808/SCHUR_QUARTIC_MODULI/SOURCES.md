# Sources and binding local inputs

## Primary literature

1. Yu. Tschinkel and Zh. Zhang, *Stable equivariant birationalities of
   cubic and degree 14 Fano threefolds*, arXiv:2409.08392, especially
   diagram (3.2), Proposition 3.3, and Proposition 4.1.  Diagram (3.2),
   rather than the later no-name argument, supplies the fibrewise kernel
   map used in the degree calculation.

2. A. Kuznetsov, *Derived categories of cubic and V14 threefolds*,
   arXiv:math/0303037, especially Propositions 2.11 and 2.15 and Theorem
   2.17.  These identify the common quartic determinantal hypersurface,
   its rank-four open set, and the Pfaffian--Grassmannian flop.

3. A. Iliev and D. Markushevich, *The Abel--Jacobi map for a cubic
   threefold and periods of Fano threefolds of degree 14*, Documenta
   Math. 5 (2000), 23--47, especially Theorems 1.2 and 5.2.  Theorem 5.2
   is explicitly a statement for a generic cubic and a generic
   Abel--Jacobi value.

4. C. Li, Y. Lin, L. Pertusi, and X. Zhao, *Higher dimensional moduli
   spaces on Kuznetsov components of Fano threefolds*, J. Reine Angew.
   Math. 832 (2026), 81--151, arXiv:2406.09124, especially Theorem 7.10,
   Proposition 8.5, and Example 8.8.  The results concern general
   Abel--Jacobi fibres; they do not identify the distinguished twisted
   zero fibre.

5. B. Lehmann and S. Tanimoto, *Rational curves on prime Fano
   threefolds of index 1*, J. Algebraic Geom. 30 (2021), 151--188,
   arXiv:1808.05590, especially Lemma 7.5 and Theorem 7.6.  Their
   quartic-component theorem assumes the Fano threefold is general in
   moduli and therefore cannot be specialized formally to the Klein
   \(V_{14}\).
