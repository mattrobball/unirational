## Primary geometric inputs

1. A. Kuznetsov, *Derived categories of cubic and V14 threefolds*,
   arXiv:math/0303037.  The two Palatini resolutions and the transform
   relation used to identify the degree-four component.
2. F. Flamini and E. Sernesi, *The curve of lines on a prime Fano threefold
   of genus 8*, arXiv:0811.0523.  The kernel-line closed embedding and
   Palatini line component.
3. Yu. Tschinkel and Zh. Zhang, *Stable equivariant birationalities of cubic
   and degree 14 Fano threefolds*, arXiv:2409.08392.  Equivariance of the
   Pfaffian--Grassmannian diagram and compatibility with twisting.

The present packet adds the nonsplit gerbe/quaternion descent of the
`25 x 21` companion map and the arithmetic invariant audit.  It does not
claim a new pointlessness theorem.
