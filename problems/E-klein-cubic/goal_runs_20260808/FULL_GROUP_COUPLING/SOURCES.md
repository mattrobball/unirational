# Sources and dependencies

No external classification theorem beyond the standard simplicity and order
of `PSL(2,11)` is used.

Internal inputs:

1. `../RANK4_GLOBAL/RANK3_KLEIN_COVER_BOUNDARY.md` — identification of the
   surviving rank-three Kummer cover with the Klein monomial cover.
2. `../EQUIVARIANT_LOCALIZATION/THEOREM.md` — the fixed-vertex and first
   exceptional-layer constraints whose possible iteration motivates the
   tangent-weight countercycle.

All new group-order, lattice, residue-character, double-coset-count, and
tangent-cycle checks are replayed by `verify.py`.
