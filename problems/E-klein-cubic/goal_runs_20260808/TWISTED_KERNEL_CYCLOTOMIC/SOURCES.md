# Primary sources and scope

- G. Bayarmagnai, *Essential dimension of some twists of `mu_(p^n)`*,
  RIMS Kokyuroku Bessatsu B4 (2007), 145--151.  Theorem 3.2 is an upper
  bound; the odd-prime equality used as motivation is conjectural.
  <https://repository.kulib.kyoto-u.ac.jp/dspace/bitstream/2433/174166/1/B04_010.pdf>

- A. Merkurjev, *Essential dimension: a survey*, Section 8b.  This records
  the permutation-presentation construction and Ruozzi's conjecture, and
  states the same-prime hypothesis under which the conjecture is proved.
  <https://www.math.ucla.edu/~merkurev/papers/survey-update3.pdf>

- B. Totaro, *The Chow ring of a classifying space*, in *Algebraic
  K-theory* (Seattle, 1997), Proc. Sympos. Pure Math. 67 (1999), 249--281.
  This supplies the approximation definition used for Chow groups of finite
  group schemes.  The presentation `CH^*(B mu_n)=Z[c]/(nc)` is the standard
  diagonalizable-group calculation.
  <https://www.math.ucla.edu/~totaro/papers/public_html/chowclass.pdf>

- D. Kaur and Z. Reichstein, *Essential Dimension of Small Finite Groups*,
  arXiv:2407.21449.  The constant group `C11:C5` remains in the range `3--4`;
  this source does not prove the twisted-kernel lower bound.
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.21449>

- Y. Burda, *Coverings over Tori and Topological Approach to Klein's
  Resolvent Problem*, Transform. Groups 17 (2012), 921--951, Theorems 7 and
  16.  For an unramified cover of a complex torus, the exact inducing
  dimension is the rank of its monodromy group.  Applied after splitting,
  this gives one, not four.
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/1107.3444>

- J. Kollar and Z. Zhuang, *Essential dimension of isogenies*, Pure Appl.
  Math. Q. 22 (2026), 675--690, especially Theorem 2 and Lemma 16.  The
  lower-bound theorem concerns complex abelian varieties, not affine
  algebraic tori; the general cyclic-cover bound in Lemma 16 gives one after
  the degree-five splitting base change.
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.15362>

- B. Farb, M. Kisin, and J. Wolfson, *Essential dimension via prismatic
  cohomology*, Duke Math. J. 173 (2024), 2891--2950.  Its finite-cover lower
  bounds are `p`-essential.  The degree-five splitting extension is
  prime-to-`11`, so these theorems do not retain the mixed-prime descent
  datum in this example.
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.05534>

The cyclotomic generator, the self-isogeny identification, and the
low-codimension descent calculation are proved directly in `THEOREM.md`.
