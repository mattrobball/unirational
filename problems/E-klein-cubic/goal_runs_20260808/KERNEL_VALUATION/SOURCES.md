# Primary sources and exact use

- G. Bayarmagnai, *Essential dimension of some twists of `mu_(p^n)`*,
  RIMS Kokyuroku Bessatsu B4 (2007), 145--151.  Theorem 3.2 supplies the
  upper bound; the odd-prime equality is explicitly a conjecture.
  <https://repository.kulib.kyoto-u.ac.jp/dspace/bitstream/2433/174166/1/B04_010.pdf>

- R. Loetscher, M. MacDonald, A. Meyer, Z. Reichstein, *Essential
  p-dimension of algebraic tori*, J. Reine Angew. Math. 677 (2013), 1--13.
  Proposition 6.1 computes the prime-local value for a form of `mu_(p^n)`.
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/0910.5574>

- R. Loetscher, *A fiber dimension theorem for essential and canonical
  dimension*, Compos. Math. 149 (2013), 148--174.  Example 5.12 proves
  incompressibility of an anisotropic torus with cyclic prime-power splitting
  field.  Theorem 5.1 and Corollary 5.5 show exactly where `p`-exhaustivity is
  required; they do not supply the missing ordinary lower bound for `A`.
  <https://doi.org/10.1112/S0010437X12000565>

The coprime-torus lemma, the one-variable Kummer class, and its reducible
special fiber are proved directly in `THEOREM.md` and checked by `verify.py`.
