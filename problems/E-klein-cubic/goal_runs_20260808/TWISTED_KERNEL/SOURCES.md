# Primary sources

- G. Bayarmagnai, *Essential dimension of some twists of `mu_(p^n)`*,
  RIMS Kôkyûroku Bessatsu B4 (2007), 145--151.  The odd-prime formula is a
  conjecture; Theorem 3.2 is the upper bound.
  <https://repository.kulib.kyoto-u.ac.jp/dspace/bitstream/2433/174166/1/B04_010.pdf>
- R. Lötscher, M. MacDonald, A. Meyer, Z. Reichstein, *Essential
  p-dimension of algebraic tori*, J. Reine Angew. Math. 677 (2013),
  1--13.  Proposition 6.1 computes the `11`-essential value; the hypotheses
  of Theorem 1.1 do not hold here.
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/0910.5574>
- N. Fakhruddin, *Finite group schemes of essential dimension one*, Doc.
  Math. 25 (2020), 299--312.
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/1908.02438>
- Z. Reichstein, F. Scavia, *Essential dimension of extensions of finite
  groups by tori*, Algebraic Geometry 8 (2021), 749--769.  Its results remain
  prime-local and do not supply the ordinary mixed-prime lower bound used
  here.
  <https://content.algebraicgeometry.nl/2021-6/2021-6-023.pdf>
