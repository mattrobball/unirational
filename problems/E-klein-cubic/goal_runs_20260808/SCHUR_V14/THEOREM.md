
`C5`-fixed scheme is finite, while `V14^D10` is empty.

Therefore every rational `G`-map


the irreducible line would map constantly to the finite scheme `V14^C5`.


66 lines are exactly the complete secant graph on the 12 vertices:

```text

```

This is an all-degree base-locus theorem; it is not a degree cutoff.
