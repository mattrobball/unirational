# Status

The finite minimal-class target has an affirmative verdict: the actual Klein
principal polarization on the unpolarized product \(E^5\) has integral
Hermitian inverse, and that inverse has an explicit integral rank-one
decomposition.  Consequently \(\theta^4/4!\) is the class of an algebraic
one-cycle.

Voisin's criterion then shows that the Klein cubic has universally trivial
\(CH_0\).  Thus ordinary decomposition-of-the-diagonal theory cannot exclude
the degree-one rational-retraction branch.  Equivariant retraction existence
remains open.

The identity restriction adds one fixed-plane condition: the odd leading
minus term along every involution plane is coefficientwise divisible by that
plane's elliptic cubic. This returns the elliptic divisor to the resolved base
ideal but is compatible with separation of the plus and minus exceptional
eigenspaces; it is not an all-degree contradiction.
