# Status

`OPEN`

Finite CAS was deployed on every named bounded target isolated in this audit.
This is an inventory of the audited routes, not a theorem that no other finite
invariant can exist.  The computations proved exact circularity for the Schur
quartic, exact emptiness
for the complete `C11` degree-eight tangency target, geometric nonemptiness
and a mixed-volume bound for the degree-nine osculating target, and
characteristic-five two-residue emptiness through covariant degree forty-five.
For one fixed three-residue pattern it gives dependency-free support
exhaustion through degree forty-five and a separately labelled exact
trusted-solver replay at degree fifty.  The full-group restriction theorem
also reduces every hypothetical map to a generically finite equivariant
selfmap of the Klein cubic.  Exact localization and incidence computations
leave compatible first- and second-layer fixed-stratum states, and the
normalizer-coupled `C3`, `C5`, `C11`, and `V4` equations eliminate no
degree residue.  Superrigidity
does exclude restriction degree two, while ordinary decomposition of the
diagonal affirmatively passes in degree one.  The direct finite tests of the
equivariant diagonal also pass: genuine Sylow-fixed point orbits generate an
equivariant zero-cycle of degree one, the allowed support absorbs every fixed
elliptic component, and higher Amitsur/Brauer/descent tests vanish.  The exact
polarized retraction identity lowers degree when its residual discriminant is
square, but a theorem-forced nonsquare branch remains and is realized by an
explicit boundary countermodel.  More generally, every Galois
restriction degree from two through eleven is excluded.  Deckless non-Galois
degrees at least three and the equivariant rational-retraction branch remain
open.  The first such degree, three, has been checked through its full `S3`
resolvent, auxiliary quadratic covers, an intermediate-Jacobian sanity screen,
and all fixed graph equations; none of those individual finite tests gives a
contradiction, and no joint realization is claimed.

No result excludes all `K`-points of the generic `F55` trace cubic.  The
requested non-`PSL(2,11)`-unirationality theorem is not proved.
