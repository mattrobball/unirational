Every nonzero constant-coefficient Laurent polynomial with three distinct
support exponents has nonzero trace cubic.  Exponents are unrestricted.

This is an exact sparse-support theorem, not a support bound and not a global
pointlessness theorem.  Supports of cardinality at least four and rational
functions remain outside the packet.
