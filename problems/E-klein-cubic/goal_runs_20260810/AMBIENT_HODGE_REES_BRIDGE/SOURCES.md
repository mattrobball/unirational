# Sources

## Primary literature used in the proof

1. M. Saito, **Modules de Hodge polarisables**, *Publ. Res. Inst. Math.
   Sci.* **24** (1988), 849--995.
   DOI: `10.2977/PRIMS/1195173930`.

2. M. Saito, **Mixed Hodge Modules**, *Publ. Res. Inst. Math. Sci.* **26**
   (1990), 221--333. DOI: `10.2977/PRIMS/1195171082`.

3. M. A. A. de Cataldo and L. Migliorini, **The Hodge theory of algebraic
   maps**, *Ann. Sci. Éc. Norm. Supér.* **38** (2005), 693--750.
   DOI: `10.1016/j.ansens.2005.07.001`; arXiv: `math/0306030`.

4. M. A. A. de Cataldo and L. Migliorini, **The perverse filtration and the
   Lefschetz hyperplane theorem**, *Ann. of Math.* **171** (2010),
   2089--2113. DOI: `10.4007/annals.2010.171.2089`.

5. M. Hanamura and M. Saito, **Weight filtration on the cohomology of
   algebraic varieties**, arXiv: `math/0605603` (2006), especially Theorem 1.

## Restriction and vanishing-cycle references

6. Q. Chen, B. Dirks, and M. Saito, **Verdier specialization and restrictions
   of Hodge modules**, arXiv: `2304.13644`.

7. Q. Chen, B. Dirks, and S. Olano, **Restrictions of mixed Hodge modules
   using generalized V-filtrations**, *Adv. Math.* **499** (2026), Article
   111060. DOI: `10.1016/j.aim.2026.111060`; arXiv: `2410.09959`.

## Birational and motivic references

8. D. Abramovich, K. Karu, K. Matsuki, and J. Wlodarczyk, **Torification and
   factorization of birational maps**, *J. Amer. Math. Soc.* **15** (2002),
   531--572. DOI: `10.1090/S0894-0347-02-00396-X`; arXiv: `math/9904135`.

9. Yu. I. Manin, **Correspondences, motifs and monoidal transformations**,
   *Math. USSR-Sb.* **6** (1968), 439--470.
   DOI: `10.1070/SM1968v006n04ABEH001070`.

10. M. A. A. de Cataldo and L. Migliorini, **The Chow motive of semismall
    resolutions**, *Math. Res. Lett.* **11** (2004), 151--170.
    DOI: `10.4310/MRL.2004.v11.n2.a2`.

11. M. A. A. de Cataldo and L. Migliorini, **The projectors of the
    decomposition theorem are motivic**, *Math. Res. Lett.* **22** (2015),
    1061--1088. DOI: `10.4310/MRL.2015.v22.n4.a6`; arXiv: `1401.3705`.

12. M. A. A. de Cataldo and L. Migliorini, **The hard Lefschetz theorem and
    the topology of semismall maps**, *Ann. Sci. Éc. Norm. Supér.* **35**
    (2002), 759--772.

## Rees, coniveau, and intermediate-Jacobian context

13. S. S. Abhyankar and W. J. Heinzer, **Rees valuations**, *Proc. Indian
    Acad. Sci. Math. Sci.* **122** (2012), 525--546.
    DOI: `10.1007/s12044-012-0081-8`; expanded arXiv version: `1508.05697`.

14. C. Voisin, **On the coniveau of rationally connected threefolds**,
    *Geom. Topol.* **26** (2022), 2731--2772.
    DOI: `10.2140/gt.2022.26.2731`; arXiv: `2010.05275`.

15. J. D. Achter, S. Casalaina-Martin, and C. Vial, **On descending
    cohomology geometrically**, *Compos. Math.* **153** (2017), 1446--1478.
    DOI: `10.1112/S0010437X17007151`.
