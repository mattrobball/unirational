# `V14^{S3}` and `V14^{D10}`: the two missing inputs for route 2

The boxed SPIN-LINKING LEMMA of `KLEIN_SPIN_COMPLEX.md` §7 has two named
routes past it. Route 2 (second-generation strata) needed two loci that no
sealed packet had measured: the fixed loci of the nonabelian subgroups `S_3`
and `D_10` of `G = PSL(2,F_11)` on the `V14` twin. This file measures them
exactly and as schemes, together with `V14^{A_4}` and `V14^{A_5}`, which came
free in the same framework and turned out to be load-bearing.

So `V14` contains exactly **220 points with stabiliser `S_3`** (two `G`-orbits

Route 2 in one line each: on the **132 `D_10`-points** it converts into an
unconditional theorem (Thm V1 below — the sign-point stays in the base

second-generation strata there can never be links in the chain the boxed
lemma asks for.

## 1. What was measured, and against which model

`V14 = Gr(2,U) cap P(M)` in the sealed FIX-IX model
(`goal_runs_after_c53d89a/FIX_IX_SEAL`, exit `FIX-IX-SEAL-PASS`): `U` is the
6-dimensional even Weil (spin) representation of `SL(2,11)` generated by
`T6 = diag(z^{j^2})` and `S6 = (1/gauss)(z^{ij} + z^{-ij})`, and `M` is the
10-dimensional `10'`-summand of `Lambda^2 U`, the column space of the
isotypic projector. `G = PSL(2,11)` acts on `M` because `Lambda^2` kills
`-I`, and `V14` is cut out of `P(M) = P^9` by the 15 Pluecker quadrics
restricted to `M`.

`verify_v14_s3_d10.py` rebuilds that model with the seal's own field layer,
generators, closure, projector and Pluecker-restriction routine, and adds
only the subgroup layer. Three regression numbers, recomputed through the new
pipeline in **every** mode, pin it to the seal:

```text
REG SIGPLUS  dim 2 degree 6    the smooth genus-1 sextic  E_sigma
REG SIGMINUS dim 1 degree 2    the two isolated sigma-points
REG V14      dim 4 degree 14   V14 itself
```

(Macaulay2 `dim` is the affine dimension of the cone: projective dimensions
1, 0, 3. These are exactly the sealed values.)

The subgroup lattice is also recomputed and checked against Dickson: 55
`C_3`, 66 `C_5`, **110 `S_3` in two `G`-classes of 55**, 66 `D_10` in one
class, 55 `A_4` in one class, 22 `A_5` in two classes of 11. The two classes
of `S_3` matter: `N_G(S_3) = D_12` has order 12, so each class has
`660/12 = 55` members, and both classes were measured separately.

## 2. The splitting lemma

> **Lemma 1.** Let `H <= G` be a finite subgroup acting linearly on `M`,
> over a field of characteristic 0 or prime to `|H|`. Then, as schemes,
>
> ```text
> P(M)^H  =  | |_chi  P(M_chi),     M_chi = { m : h.m = chi(h) m  for all h },
> ```
>
> the disjoint union over the linear characters `chi` of `H`; hence
>
> ```text
> V14^H  =  | |_chi ( V14 cap P(M_chi) ),
> ```
>
> each piece cut out by the 15 Pluecker quadrics restricted to `M_chi`.

*Proof.* A point `[m]` is `H`-fixed iff the line `k.m` is `H`-stable iff `H`
acts on it through a linear character. Scheme-theoretically, the fixed locus
of one diagonalisable `g` is `V(2x2 minors of [v ; g.v])`, whose ideal in
eigencoordinates is `((lam_i - lam_j) x_i x_j)` — the radical ideal of the
union of the eigen-subspaces; intersecting over generators of `H` gives the
joint eigenspaces, which are the `M_chi`. Intersecting with `V14` is a
scheme-theoretic intersection on both sides. `QED`

Linear characters used: `S_3 -> {triv, sign}`, `D_10 -> {triv, sign}`,
`A_5 -> {triv}` (perfect), `A_4 -> {triv, omega, omega^2}`.

The two `omega`-characters of `A_4` are handled **without adjoining
`zeta_3`**: they span the rational `A_4`-stable plane
`N = M^{V_4} (-) M^{A_4}` (dimensions 4 and 2, so `dim N = 2`), and inside
`P(N) = P^1` the two `omega`-eigenpoints are cut by the rational binary
quadratic `det[v ; r.v]`, `r` of order 3. So `V14 cap (P(M_omega) u
P(M_omega^2)) = V(quadrics|_N , det[v ; r.v])` in `P^1`, entirely over the
base field.

The dimensions of the pieces are forced by character theory. With
`chi_{10'} = (10, 2, 1, 0, -1, -1)` on projective orders `(1,2,3,5,6,11)`:

| `H` | `dim M_triv` | `dim M_sign` | other |
|---|---|---|---|
| `S_3` | 3 (`P^2`) | 1 (a point of `P^9`) | — |
| `D_10` | 2 (`P^1`) | **0** | — |
| `A_4` | 2 (`P^1`) | — | `omega`, `omega^2`: 1 + 1 |
| `A_5` | 1 (a point) | — | — |

Every one of these dimensions is verified computationally against the
character-theoretic prediction in every mode.

## 3. The measurement

### 3.1 Rank certificates (exact, no Groebner basis)

If the 15 restricted quadrics span the **whole** space of quadratic forms on
`M_chi`, the homogeneous ideal contains every quadric, so its saturation is
the unit ideal and `V14 cap P(M_chi)` is empty as a scheme. This is a pure
rank computation and it settles five of the nine pieces outright, in all four
modes:

| piece | `dim M_chi` | quadrics span | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| `S_3` sign | 1 | 1 of 1 | **empty** |
| `D_10` triv | 2 | 3 of 3 | **empty** |
| `D_10` sign | 0 | — | **empty** |
| `A_4` `omega`-pair | 2 | 3 of 3 | **empty** |
| `A_5` triv (both classes) | 1 | 1 of 1 | **empty** |
| `S_3` triv | 3 | 4 of 6 | not decided by rank |
| `A_4` triv | 2 | 2 of 3 | not decided by rank |

### 3.2 Macaulay2 on the two undecided pieces

```text
PIECE S3_c0_triv  empty false   dim 1 degree 2   smooth true    (radical, ncomp 2 at 397)
PIECE S3_c1_triv  empty false   dim 1 degree 2   smooth true    (radical, ncomp 2 at 397)
PIECE A4_c0_triv  empty false   dim 1 degree 1   smooth true    (radical, ncomp 1)
```

Affine `dim 1` = projective dimension 0. `smooth true` is the Jacobian
criterion `saturate(I + minors(codim I, jacobian I)) = (1)`; for a
0-dimensional scheme over a perfect field it is exactly geometric
reducedness, and it is available over `K` where Macaulay2's `radical` and
`primaryDecomposition` are not (the same limitation the seal recorded). So
each `S_3` locus is **2 distinct geometric points** and each `A_4` locus is
**1 reduced point**.

### 3.3 The same answer from the definition, in the ambient `P^9`

Independently of Lemma 1 and of all character theory, for `H = <g,h>` the
ideal

```text
I_{V14}  +  minors(2, [x ; g.x])  +  minors(2, [x ; h.x])   in  k[x0..x9]
```

is the scheme-theoretic fixed locus. Saturated:

```text
AMBIENT S3_c0  empty false  dim 1 degree 2  smooth true
AMBIENT S3_c1  empty false  dim 1 degree 2  smooth true
AMBIENT D10_c0 empty true
AMBIENT A4_c0  empty false  dim 1 degree 1  smooth true
AMBIENT A5_c0  empty true
AMBIENT A5_c1  empty true
```

Same verdicts, same degrees, two independent code paths — and this one runs
in characteristic 0 as well: over `K` the same six lines come out with the
same numbers (`AMBIENT S3_c0 dim 1 degree 2 smooth true`, `AMBIENT D10_c0
empty true`, and so on). It is the slow part of the char-0 mode: saturating
105 quadrics in 10 variables over a degree-10 number field takes about a
quarter of an hour, against seconds for the character pieces.

## 4. Component data

**`V14^{S3}` (both classes).** Zero-dimensional, degree 2, geometrically
reduced: two distinct points, no curve component, no embedded structure. They
are not both rational over the base field in general — at `p = 397` and
`p = 353` they split into two rational points; at `p = 199` they form a single
closed point of degree 2. Hence over `K = Q(zeta_11)` they are **not** both
`K`-rational (a `K`-rational pair would split at every good prime); they are
conjugate over a quadratic extension of `K`, while their sum is defined over
`K`.

*Stabilisers.* Measured directly at `p = 397` and `p = 353` (where the points
are rational) by acting with all 660 elements of `G`: `Stab_G(y)` has order 6
and **equals** the given `S_3`; the set-stabiliser of the pair has order 12.
Derived in general, valid in characteristic 0: the subgroups of `PSL(2,11)`
containing `S_3` are `S_3`, `D_12`, `A_5`, `G`; `V14^{D_12} = empty` (sealed),
`V14^{A_5} = empty` (measured here), and `V14^G <= V14^{A_5}`. Hence
`Stab_G(y) = S_3` exactly, the orbit has length `660/6 = 110`, and
`N_G(S_3) = D_12` — which stabilises the pair, since `y` determines its
`S_3` — permutes the two points **transitively** (a point stabiliser of index
2 in `D_12`). This is the exact analogue, one level up, of the sealed picture
in which `D_12` swaps the two isolated `C_6`-points of `V14^sigma`.

*Counting.* 55 subgroups per class, 2 points each, all distinct (a point
determines its `S_3`): 110 points per class forming one orbit of length 110.
**Two `G`-orbits, 220 points in all.**

*Incidence with the sealed sextics.* `M^{S_3, triv}` lies in the `+1`
eigenspace of each of the three involutions of `S_3` (verified in every
mode), and `V14 cap P(M^{sigma,+}) = E_sigma` is the sealed smooth genus-1
sextic. So each of the 220 points lies on `E_rho` for **all three**
involutions `rho` of its `S_3` — the three sextics of an `S_3` share these two
points — and none of them is one of the isolated `sigma`-points (those have
stabiliser exactly `C_6 != S_3`).

**`V14^{D10}`.** Empty, both character pieces, hence for all 66 subgroups.
The trivial piece is a `P^1` on which the 15 Pluecker quadrics already span
all three binary quadratic forms; the sign piece is 0-dimensional. No
Groebner basis is needed for either.

**`V14^{A4}`.** One reduced point per `A_4`. `Stab_G` measured directly at
397, 199 and 353: order 12, equal to the given `A_4`, orbit length 55. (Same
derivation as above: the only subgroups containing `A_4` are `A_4`, `A_5`,
`G`, and the last two have empty fixed loci.) One `G`-orbit of 55 points.
The point lies on `E_sigma` for each of the three involutions of its `V_4`.
Note this is a genuinely non-automatic answer, as the spin packet warned:
`A_4` has faithful 3-dimensional representations and `V14` is a threefold.

**`V14^{A5}`.** Empty for both classes; the trivial piece is a single point
of `P^9` and it is not on `V14`. Consequently `V14^G = empty` too.

## 5. What this does to route 2

Notation as in `KLEIN_SPIN_COMPLEX.md` §6-7: `x` one of the 352 incidence
points, `K = Stab_G(x)` in `{S_3, D_10}`, `E_x = P(T_x) = P^4` the exceptional
divisor of the blowup of `P(U)` at `x`, `s_x = P(T_x^{sign})` the sign-point,
and for each involution `rho` of `K` the second-generation stratum
`P(T_x^{rho,-1}) = P^2 <= E_x`, pointwise `rho`-fixed, rational, and passing
through `s_x` (K5).

> **Theorem V1 (the 132 `D_10` sign-points are base locus).** Let
> `phi : P(U) --> V14` be any `G`-equivariant rational map and `x` one of the
> 132 incidence points with `Stab_G(x) = D_10`. Then the induced rational map
> `Bl_x P(U) --> V14` is **undefined at `s_x`**.

*Proof.* Suppose it is defined at `s_x`. Each of the five strata
`P(T_x^{rho,-1})` is irreducible and contains `s_x`, so it meets the domain of
definition in a dense open set; its image is an irreducible rational (hence
RCC) subvariety of `V14^rho`, and `V14^rho` = smooth genus-1 sextic + 2
points contains **no rational curve** (sealed), so the image is a single
point, necessarily the value at `s_x`. All five give the same value `y`, so
`Stab_G(y)` contains all five involutions of `D_10`, i.e. `D_10` itself (any
two distinct involutions already generate it). But `V14^{D_10} = empty`. `QED`

This does not close the box, but it is new and unconditional, and it extends
Theorem K4 one blowup deeper: over 132 of the 352 forced points the
resolution is *not* finished after the first blowup — a third generation is
forced, exactly where route 2 lives.

> **Theorem V2 (the 220 `S_3` sign-points are pinned).** Let `x` be one of the
> 220 points with `Stab_G(x) = S_3`. If `Bl_x P(U) --> V14` is defined at
> `s_x`, its value is one of the **two** points of `V14^{S_3(x)}`. That value
> has stabiliser exactly `S_3`, lies on the genus-1 sextic `E_rho` of each of
> the three involutions of `S_3(x)`, and is **different from all three carrier
> points** at `x` — an `S_3`-point lies on 3 planes, one per involution of its
> `S_3`, and their carriers have stabiliser exactly `C_6` (Thm K2).

*Proof.* The same contraction argument gives a common value `y` with
`Stab_G(y) >= S_3`, so `y in V14^{S_3}`, a set of exactly two points; §4 gives
its stabiliser, its position on the sextics, and `S_3 != C_6`. `QED`

> **Theorem V3 (no second-generation link).** Fix one of the 352 points `x`,
> `K = Stab_G(x)`. No chain of irreducible RCC subvarieties, each pointwise
> fixed by an involution of `G` and consecutive members meeting, can contain a
> first-generation carrier at `x` together with the second-generation strata
> `P(T_x^{rho,-1})` and `P(T_x^{rho',-1})` of two distinct involutions
> `rho != rho'` of `K`.

*Proof.* Every member of such a chain is RCC and pointwise fixed by an
involution, hence contracted to a point of the corresponding `V14^iota`;
consecutive members meet, so the whole chain has a single image `y`. The two
distinct second-generation strata force `Stab_G(y) >= <rho, rho'> = K`, and
the carrier forces `Stab_G(y) = C_6(sigma)` exactly (Thm K2). But `K` is
`S_3` or `D_10` and neither is contained in `C_6`. `QED`

*Second proof of the `S_3` case, independent of Thm K2 and using the new
`A_5` datum.* `Stab_G(y)` would contain `<C_6(sigma), S_3>` for an involution
`sigma in S_3`. Now `sigma` centralises the `C_3` of `C_6(sigma)` and inverts
the `C_3` of `S_3`, so those two `C_3`s are distinct; the subgroup therefore
has at least two Sylow 3-subgroups **and** an element of order 6. In the
subgroup lattice of `PSL(2,11)` the only subgroup with both properties is `G`
itself, and `V14^G <= V14^{A_5} = empty`. `QED`

**Net effect on route 2.** Route 2 proposed the second-generation strata as
the links joining the carriers of `Pi_sigma^eps` and `Pi_tau^delta`. Crossing
from `sigma` to `tau` needs strata of two distinct involutions, so Thm V3
rules that out at every one of the 352 points: **route 2 cannot supply the
chain the SPIN-LINKING LEMMA asks for.** What it does supply is Thm V1 — over
the 132 `D_10`-points the sign-point is itself mandatory base locus, so the
blowup tower does not stop at the first generation there. The boxed lemma
remains open; route 1 (multiplicity, Thm 7.4 of the engine) is the remaining
named route, and it is also the only one that can discharge the "all faithful
spin sources" quantifier the headline needs.

**Source against target, per subgroup.** Dividing the packet's counts by the
number of subgroups (220 points over 110 `S_3`s, 132 over 66 `D_10`s) gives
the source side of this table; this file supplies the target side.

| `H` | `P(U)^H` | `V14^H` |
|---|---|---|
| `S_3` | 2 points | **2 points** |
| `D_10` | 2 points | **empty** |

The `S_3` agreement carries no map — Theorem K4 puts all 352 source points in
the indeterminacy locus of every equivariant rational map, so nothing is being
sent anywhere — but it is why route 2 cannot close by emptiness there, and
Thm V3 has to do the work instead. The `D_10` asymmetry is exactly what
Theorem V1 runs on.

## 6. Verification standard

Matches FIX-IX-SEAL exactly:

* **exact characteristic 0** over `K = Q(z)/Phi_11`, the field of the
  explicit even-Weil model (entries in `Z[z, 1/11]`), with `Fraction`
  arithmetic and no floating point anywhere;
* **two independent primes** `p = 397` and `p = 199` (`p = 1 mod 11`), plus a
  third prime `p = 353` as an end-to-end replay;
* every locus decided **twice** in each mode: by an exact rank certificate in
  python and by Macaulay2 saturation; and once more, for the whole `V14^H`,
  from the definition in the ambient `P^9` via `minors(2, [x ; g.x])`, which
  uses no character theory at all — this third path is run in all four modes,
  characteristic 0 included;
* the three sealed regression numbers (`sigma`-sextic, `sigma`-points, `V14`
  itself) recomputed through the new pipeline in every mode, including `K`
  — note that the ambient `dim 4 degree 14` is obtained here over `K`,
  whereas the seal ran the direct ambient Groebner basis only at the primes;
* the subgroup lattice of `PSL(2,11)` recomputed and checked (counts and
  numbers of `G`-classes) rather than assumed;
* stabilisers of the nonempty loci measured directly by acting with all 660
  elements of `G` wherever the points are rational (397, 353 for `S_3`; all
  three primes for `A_4`), and derived in characteristic 0 from the subgroup
  lattice plus `V14^{D_12} = empty` (sealed) and `V14^{A_5} = empty` (new).

No sampling and no numerical approximation. Every emptiness claim is an
ideal-membership statement (`saturation = unit ideal`) or a rank equality; the
only search anywhere is the **exhaustive** enumeration of `P(M_chi)(F_p)` used
at the primes to exhibit the points and measure their stabilisers, and no
verdict depends on it.
