## 3. The two walls, precisely

1. **No effective degree bound.** Finite generation of the covariant
   module is abstract; nothing bounds the degree of a minimal landing
   covariant. Every negative argument must therefore be uniform in
   degree; the terminal verdict shows the localized machinery cannot
   supply that uniformity (the plus-side jet freedom outruns the
   coupled ladder at every level).
2. **No algebraization.** Jet and level solvability never implies a
   polynomial map (T5 gate; C1's calibration: a ladder that cannot
   detect a known death certifies nothing by extending). The explicit
   FIX-D2 jet survivors and the (1,7) witnesses are boundary data, not
   germs of maps, until an equivariant algebraization statement exists.

Any decisive continuation must break one of these. Everything else is
refinement.
