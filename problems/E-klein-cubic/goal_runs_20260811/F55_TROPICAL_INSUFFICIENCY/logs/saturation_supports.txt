PART 3 -- scope, stated honestly
  Every support run here with the authoritative twist e_2 saturates
  to (1).  No support with I_S : m_S^inf != (1) is known for that
  twist, and producing one would BE a K-point of the F55 trace
  cubic -- it would settle Problem E positively, not negatively.
  The gate is an exact per-support decision procedure.  It is NOT a
  classification of supports: the universally quantified statement
  'I_S : m_S^inf = (1) for every primitive finite support S' is
  equivalent to the F55 headline itself (COVERAGE_RELATION.md).
