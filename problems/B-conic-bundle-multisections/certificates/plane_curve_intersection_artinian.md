# Plane-curve intersection: the Artinian bridge

Let `K` be a field and let `f,g ∈ K[u,v]` be the two dehomogenized equations on one
standard chart of a projective plane intersection.  Regard them first as polynomials in `u`
over `K[v]`.  If they are coprime over `K(v)`, their `u`-resultant is a nonzero polynomial
`R_v(v)`.  The Sylvester adjugate identity gives

`a(u,v) f(u,v) + b(u,v) g(u,v) = R_v(v)`.

Thus `R_v(v)` vanishes in `K[u,v]/(f,g)`, so the class of `v` is algebraic over `K`.
Repeating the argument after swapping `u` and `v` makes the class of `u` algebraic.  Because
the quotient is generated by these two integral elements, it is finite-dimensional over `K`,
and therefore Artinian.

For two homogeneous plane curves with no common component, coprimality over the opposite
coordinate fraction field is the standard algebraic formulation of the same condition on each
nonempty affine chart.  Applying the affine argument on the three standard charts proves that
their projective scheme-theoretic intersection is locally Artinian.

`PlaneCurveIntersectionArtinian.lean` formalizes the commutative-algebra argument.  In particular
it formalizes:

- the Sylvester identity as an explicit coordinate eliminant;
- both-coordinate resultant nonvanishing as Artinianness of `K[u,v]/(f,g)`;
- the fraction-field coprimality criterion for resultant nonvanishing;
- gluing an open cover of such affine intersections into a locally Artinian scheme; and
- finite underlying fibers of locally finite type morphisms as locally Artinian scheme fibers.

`BiprojectiveTwoEquationAffine.lean` and `TargetRelationGenericFiberArtinian.lean` now formalize
the target-specific projective bookkeeping as well:

- the global complete intersection `V(F,G)` restricts on every product chart to the sum of the
  two principal chart ideals;
- after base change to a point `x` of the first projective factor, each of the three
  second-factor charts is exactly
  `Spec(κ(x)[u,v]/(f_j,g_j))`, with an exact range formula;
- those three ranges cover the whole scheme-theoretic fiber; and
- chartwise ordered fraction-field coprimality implies `IsLocallyArtinian` for the whole fiber.

For the target relation, `G = rename Sum.inr H`, and
`fstBaseChangedChartEquation_rename_inr` identifies `g_j` with the `j`-th dehomogenization of
`H` after mapping coefficients to `κ(x)`.  The final generic-fiber theorem is
`targetRelation_genericFiber_isLocallyArtinian_of_chartwiseCoprime`.

The exact remaining algebraic boundary is now smaller and explicit.  One must derive the six
chart certificates packaged by `HasTargetRelationFstFiberChartCoprimality`: on each of three
charts, a positive eliminated degree and coprimality over the opposite-coordinate fraction field,
in both coordinate orders.  A natural global proof would show that an irreducible homogeneous
`H` cannot divide the generic specialized cubic; Gauss/denominator clearing would turn such a
divisibility into a whole second fiber of `F`, contradicting the existing smoothness-based
no-whole-fiber theorem.  That global-to-chart divisibility descent is not presently formalized
here or supplied by a projective Bezout theorem in Mathlib.
