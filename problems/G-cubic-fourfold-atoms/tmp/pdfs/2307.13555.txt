                                                         QUANTUM COHOMOLOGY OF BLOWUPS

                                                                               HIROSHI IRITANI

                                                 Abstract. We prove a decomposition theorem of the quantum cohomology D-
                                                 module of the blowup X
                                                                      e = BlZ X of a smooth projective variety X along a smooth
                                                 subvariety Z. The main tools we use are shift operators and Fourier analysis for
                                                 equivariant quantum cohomology.

                                             Let X be a smooth projective variety over C and let Z ⊂ X be a smooth subvariety
                                          of codimension r ≥ 2. It is well-known that the cohomology of the blowup X  e of X
                                          along Z decomposes additively as

                                                                      H ∗ (X) = H ∗ (X) ⊕ H ∗ (Z)⊕(r−1) .
                                          The goal of the present paper is to prove an analogous (but multiplicative) decom-
                                          position for quantum cohomology rings (or F -manifolds); the decomposition also
                                          lifts to a formal decomposition of quantum cohomology D-modules.

                                          Novikov ring. The quantum D-module QDM(X) is the module H ∗ (X) ⊗ C[z][[Q, τ ]]

   (4) Coates-Lutz-Shafi [12] obtained the I-function of a blowup along a complete

   (5) Kontsevich has given several talks [51, 52, 53] on joint project with Lud-

decomposition of quantum connections for blowups (similar to Theorem 1.1) and
discovered remarkable applications to birational geometry. They, along with [36],
also highlighted the unique reconstructibility of genus-zero Gromov-Witten invari-
ants of Xe from those of X and Z, based on this decomposition theorem. We describe
the initial condition for this reconstruction in §5.8.
1.2. Equivariant quantum cohomology and Fourier transformation. We
prove the decomposition theorem (Theorem 1.1) using Fourier analysis on the equi-
variant quantum cohomology of the master space W = BlZ×{0} (X × P1 ), the blowup
of X × P1 along Z × {0}. Both X and its blowup Xe arise as the Geometric Invariant
Theory (GIT) quotient of W by the C× -action on the P1 -factor (see §3.1). Tele-
man [75] conjectured a relationship between equivariant quantum cohomology and
quantum cohomology of the GIT quotients in terms of the Seidel representation.

Remark 1.15. We can view the (r − 1) copies of QDM(Z) as wall-crossing terms
for quantum D-modules. The method in this paper could be applied to more gen-
eral GIT variations or symplectic birational cobordisms [32, 38]. It would be also
interesting to understand the relationship between our results/conjectures and the
works [81, 26, 25] on gauged Gromov-Witten theory and quantum Kirwan maps.
1.4. Plan of the paper. In §2, we recall the definition of quantum D-modules,
Givental cone, twisted Gromov-Witten invariants and shift operators. In particular,
we introduce the extended shift action of N1T (X) that combines the shift operators
and the Novikov variables. In §3, we collect basic geometric facts about the master
space W of blowups. The content of §4 is the technical core of the paper: we show
that discrete/continuous Fourier transforms of the equivariant J-function lie in the
Givental cone of the GIT quotients or T -fixed loci. In §5, we introduce a completion
of the equivariant quantum D-module of W and prove the decomposition theorem.
