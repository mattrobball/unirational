Remark 1.3 (mirror symmetry). The b        Γ-integral structure had (implicitly) appeared for a
long time in the study of mirror symmetry before it was defined in [42, 46]. Under mir-
ror symmetry of Calabi-Yau manifolds, the quantum differential equation corresponds to the
Picard-Fuchs differential equations satisfied by periods of the mirror family, and we can par-
tially see the b
               Γ-class in the asymptotics of periods near the large-complex structure limit.
Libgober [52] introduced the (inverse) Γ-class
                                         b       based on the observation of Hosono et. al. [40]
that certain combinations of Chern numbers and ζ values appear in solutions of the mirror
Picard-Fuchs equations. Hosono [39] stated a conjecture equating periods of mirrors of com-
plete intersections with explicit hypergeometric series and the Γ-class
                                                                 b      is hidden in the series.
We also refer the reader to [38, 9, 59] for related works. It has been checked in a number of
cases that the Γ-integral
                b          structure corresponds to a natural integral structure on the mirror
side [42, 44]. Regarding the compatibility with mirror symmetry, an approach based on the
SYZ picture and tropical geometry has been proposed in [1] recently.
