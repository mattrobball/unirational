# Kummer quartic double solids

## 1. Exact literature boundary

Tschinkel--Zhang study the rational Kummer quartic double solids attached
to

\[
C_1:y^2=x(x^4-1),\qquad C_2:y^2=x^5+1.
\]

They write the first model explicitly as

\[
X_1=\{w^2=x_1^4+x_2^4+x_3^4+x_4^4-4i x_1x_2x_3x_4\}
\subset\mathbf P(2,1,1,1,1),
\]

and record

\[
\operatorname{Aut}(X_1)=C_2.(C_2^4\rtimes S_4),\qquad
\operatorname{Aut}(X_2)=C_2.(C_2^4\rtimes C_5).
\]

For a smooth model obtained by resolving the 16 nodes, their Theorem 4
classifies the Condition-(A) actions with a nonzero third Amitsur
obstruction: exactly the groups containing one of two explicitly embedded
`Q_8` subgroups. Those actions are already known not to be (projectively)
`G`-unirational.

Therefore the quaternionic rows are **ALREADY-DECIDED**, not targets for a
new fixed-stratum theorem.

## 2. Why the deck involution is not enough

The deck involution is central, but its fixed divisor is the Kummer
surface. On the standard resolution this is a K3 surface carrying many
rational curves, including the 16 exceptional curves over the nodes and
the 16 trope curves. Consequently the repository's original central
criterion does not apply.

The residual-RCC theorem asks a sharper question:

> Does the residual group preserve any irreducible RCC subvariety of the
> resolved Kummer surface?

Transitivity on the visible `16+16` configuration is not sufficient. A
Kummer K3 generally has additional rational curves, and proving absence of
**all** residual-stable rational curves is a lattice-and-effective-cone
problem, not a finite permutation check on nodes and tropes.

## 3. Candidate `X_1` actions

### 3.1 Quaternionic actions

Status: **ALREADY-DECIDED**.

The explicit `Q_8` actions in formulas (4.3) and (4.4) of
Tschinkel--Zhang satisfy Condition (A) but have nonzero `Am^3`. A geometric
fixed-network proof could be interesting expositoryly, but it would not
meet the requested novelty criterion.

### 3.2 Deck times coordinate `S_4`

Consider the explicit subgroup generated by the deck involution and the
coordinate permutations. This is a natural stress test because the
residual `S_4` acts visibly on the Kummer configuration.

What is known in this audit:

- the variety is rational;
- the deck-fixed surface is non-uniruled but contains many rational curves;
- the abstract group `C_2 x S_4` contains no subgroup isomorphic to `Q_8`:
  a homomorphism `Q_8 -> S_4` cannot be injective because the
  Sylow-2 subgroups of `S_4` are dihedral, while any map
  `Q_8 -> C_2` kills the commutator/central involution, so the two
  projections cannot combine to an embedding;
- consequently the published quaternionic `Am^3` theorem does not decide
  this exact subgroup;
- no proof was located that the exact `C_2 x S_4` action is or is not
  `G`-unirational.

What remains:

1. determine Condition (A) for every abelian subgroup of this exact
   embedding;
2. compute the equivariant universal-torsor and higher-Amitsur classes for
   the exact embedding (the quaternionic shortcut is unavailable);
3. compute the `S_4`-invariant effective `(-2)` classes on the resolved
   Kummer K3;
4. decide whether any irreducible effective rational curve can be
   `S_4`-stable;
5. compute the deeper fixed locus on such a curve.

Status: **LITERATURE-STATUS-UNCERTAIN**, feasibility 48/100.

## 4. Candidate `X_2` action

The subgroup generated by the deck involution and the natural order-five
automorphism of `C_2:y^2=x^5+1` is cohomologically attractive: the Sylow
subgroups highlighted in the current paper have trivial third Bogomolov
multiplier, so the published quaternionic obstruction is absent.

This is the best remaining Kummer lane, but it is not theorem-ready. The
residual action on the full rational-curve set of the Kummer K3, Condition
(A), and the deeper fixed locus all remain to be checked.

Status: **LITERATURE-STATUS-UNCERTAIN**, feasibility 46/100.

## 5. Verdict

The Kummer examples cannot presently be killed merely by observing that no
node or trope is globally fixed. The exact missing theorem is an
equivariant Kummer-K3 statement:

> classify residual-group-stable irreducible rational curves, or prove that
> every such curve meets a deeper fixed stratum incompatible with the
> selected source survivor.

Until that effective-cone problem is solved, the geometric method does not
improve the published higher-Amitsur theorem. This is an honest negative
outcome of the audit rather than a failed finite computation.
