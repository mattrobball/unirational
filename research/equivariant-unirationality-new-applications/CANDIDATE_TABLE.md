# Ranked candidate scorecard

## Status vocabulary

- **CLOSED-HERE**: a proof is supplied in this packet.
- **OPEN-CONFIRMED**: a current primary source explicitly leaves the
  `G`-unirationality question open.
- **PARTIALLY-COVERED**: nearby birational or cohomological questions are
  decided, but the exact `G`-unirationality question is not.
- **ALREADY-DECIDED**: an existing theorem settles the relevant question.
- **LITERATURE-STATUS-UNCERTAIN**: no verdict was located, but absence from
  the searched literature is not asserted to be a proof of openness.

Here `D_{2m}` denotes the dihedral group of order `2m`. `UT` means the
equivariant universal-torsor obstruction, and `Am^*` means the full family `Am^n`, `n>=2` (including the
usual degree-two Amitsur group).

## Ranking conclusion

The single best new theorem is rank 1: the six-degenerate-fiber rational
conic bundle with `C_2 x S_3`. Rank 2 is the cleanest genuinely new use of
the residual-RCC refinement. The Fermat degree-2 action is the best
additional low-degree del Pezzo theorem. The Kummer and Burkhardt rows are
valuable primarily because the audit identifies exactly why the new
machinery does **not** yet apply.
