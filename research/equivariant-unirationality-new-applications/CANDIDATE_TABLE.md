## Ranked conclusion

The first two rows are closed theorems. The Fermat-discriminant No. 2.18 threefold is the best **next** target: it is rational, explicit, has a large finite automorphism group already computed in current literature, and fails only because the deck-fixed component is itself rational. That failure asks for exactly the higher-dimensional fixed-network extension the repository seeks.
