# Theorem: the Geiser-times-S3 Fermat degree-two del Pezzo is not weakly versal

## Statement

Let

\[
S_F=\{w^2=x^4+y^4+z^4\}\subset\mathbf P(1,1,1,2)
\]

and let `tau` be the Geiser involution

\[
\tau[x:y:z:w]=[x:y:z:-w].
\]

Let `S3` permute `x,y,z` and fix `w`, and put

\[
G_F=\langle\tau\rangle\times S_3\simeq C_2\times S_3.
\]

Then:

1. `S_F` is a smooth rational degree-two del Pezzo surface;
2. `S_F^A` is nonempty for every abelian subgroup `A <= G_F`;
3. every Sylow subgroup of `G_F` has a fixed point;
4. `S_F^{G_F}` is empty;
5. `S_F` is not weakly `G_F`-versal, hence not `G_F`-unirational.

## 1. Central fixed locus

The involution `tau` is central and

\[
S_F^\tau=B_F=\{x^4+y^4+z^4=0\}\subset\mathbf P^2.
\]

The partial derivatives are `4x^3,4y^3,4z^3`, so `B_F` is smooth.  It is a smooth plane quartic and therefore has genus three.  In particular it contains no rational curve.

## 2. Empty full fixed locus

A projective point fixed by every coordinate permutation spans a one-dimensional subrepresentation of the three-dimensional permutation representation.  The only such subrepresentation is the trivial line

\[
[1:1:1].
\]

It does not lie on the Fermat quartic because

\[
1^4+1^4+1^4=3\ne0.
\]

Any `G_F`-fixed point must be `tau`-fixed and hence lie on `B_F`.  Consequently

\[
S_F^{G_F}=B_F^{S_3}=\varnothing.
\]

## 3. Condition (A)

Let `A <= G_F` be abelian and let `B` be its projection to `S3`.  Every abelian subgroup of `S3` is trivial, `C2`, or `C3`.  Since `tau` acts trivially on `B_F`, a point of `B_F^B` is fixed by the whole graph subgroup `A`.

### Transpositions

For the transposition `(xy)`, the fixed line `x=y` meets the branch quartic in

\[
2x^4+z^4=0,
\]

which has four distinct projective roots.  Thus every order-two subgroup of `S3` fixes branch points.

### Three-cycles

Let `omega^2+omega+1=0`.  The two nontrivial eigenpoints

\[
[1:\omega:\omega^2],
\qquad
[1:\omega^2:\omega]
\]

are projectively fixed by the three-cycle and satisfy

\[
1+\omega^4+\omega^8=1+\omega+\omega^2=0.
\]

Thus every order-three subgroup fixes points on `B_F`.

It follows that

\[
S_F^A\ne\varnothing
\]

for every abelian subgroup `A <= G_F`.  Condition (A) passes.

## 4. Central obstruction

The central involution `tau` has fixed locus the genus-three curve `B_F`, and `S_F^{G_F}` is empty.  The accepted controlled central-stratum argument therefore excludes every `G_F`-equivariant rational map from a faithful linear source to `S_F`.  Dominance is not required.

Hence `S_F` is not weakly `G_F`-versal and not `G_F`-unirational.

## 5. Cohomological audit

A Sylow 2-subgroup is generated by `tau` and a transposition; it fixes one of the points on `B_F cap {x=y}`.  A Sylow 3-subgroup fixes the two displayed eigenpoints.  Thus every Sylow subgroup has a fixed point.

As in the Problem-F obstruction audit, restriction-corestriction kills the universal-torsor/ordinary Amitsur class, and the higher-Amitsur theorem kills all higher groups.  The abstract group `C2 x S3` has trivial Bogomolov multiplier.

The geometric fixed-curve obstruction therefore detects information not seen by these invariants.

## 6. Relation to existing repository examples

This is not a restatement of the order-16 Fermat actions in

```text
problems/E-klein-cubic/goal_runs_after_691986b/FIX_T34_CENTRAL_OBSTRUCTION/
```

Those groups are 2-groups and were classified action-class by action-class.  The present group has order twelve and contains a three-cycle.  The same central theorem applies, but the fixed-point and Condition-(A) audits are different.

It is also different from Duncan's classical example on the Klein degree-two del Pezzo with `Geiser x (C7 semidirect C3)`.  That example is already decided in the literature; the present Fermat/S3 action was not found decided.

## 7. Literature status

The Fermat degree-two del Pezzo and its automorphism group are classical and appear in the finite-group classifications.  Duncan's positive theorem does not cover degree two, and the cited degree-two counterexamples do not identify this exact order-12 action.  No paper through 2026-08-09 was found proving or disproving weak versality or `G_F`-unirationality for this pair.
