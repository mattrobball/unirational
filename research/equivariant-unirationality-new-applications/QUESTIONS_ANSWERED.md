# Answers to the eight required questions

## 1. Additional degree-1 or degree-2 del Pezzo path applications?

No second **verbatim** Problem-F application was found. The strongest degree-2 cases are already decided by the repository central theorem, Problem F, or the 2026 third-Amitsur obstruction. Most degree-1 actions have the anticanonical base point fixed by the group, so they cannot be non-weakly-versal; a new dominance-only obstruction would be needed.

## 2. Rational conic-bundle surfaces passing Condition (A) but not \(G\)-unirational?

Yes. For every odd \(g\ge3\),

\[
S_g:\ T_0T_1(T_0^{2g}+T_1^{2g})+T_2T_3=0
\]

with \(G_g=D_{2g}\times C_2\) passes Condition (A) but is not weakly \(G_g\)-versal. This is proved in `THEOREM_ODD_EXCEPTIONAL_CONIC_BUNDLES.md`.

## 3. Central fiber involutions on conic-bundle threefolds?

Yes, they naturally produce a discriminant or branch surface as fixed locus. If that surface is non-uniruled, the residual-RCC theorem can work after excluding residual-stable rational curves. If it is rational, as in family No. 2.18, a one-stratum central theorem is insufficient and a connected three-dimensional fixed-network theorem is needed.

## 4. Special rational Fano conic-bundle threefolds with large automorphism groups?

Yes. Abe's special No. 2.18 members with Fermat and Klein quartic discriminants are explicit and rational. The Fermat-discriminant member has the best frozen automorphism data and is the highest-ranked unresolved target.

## 5. Kummer quartic double solids despite rational curves in the fixed K3?

Possibly, but not uniformly. Q8-containing actions are already killed by \(\operatorname{Am}^3\). For a non-Q8 subgroup one must compute the residual action on the 16 exceptional and 16 trope curves. The specific test subgroup

\[
C_2^{\rm deck}\times(C_4^2\rtimes C_3)
\]

is recorded in `KUMMER_DOUBLE_SOLIDS.md`; its Condition-(A) and stable-curve audits remain open.

## 6. A second \(V_{14}\)-type index-one centralizer phenomenon?

No second case is verified. The best candidate is the Mukai–Umemura \(V_{22}\) with \(A_5\subset\operatorname{PGL}_2\). For an involution, the centralizer is \(V_4\). One exact Grassmannian fixed-scheme calculation would decide whether the \(V_{14}\) theorem applies.

## 7. Condition (A) and all known cohomology silent, but unresolved?

Before this packet, yes: the smooth quartic double solid with

\[
G=(C_7\rtimes C_3)\times C_2^{\rm deck}
\]

passed Condition (A), had vanishing equivariant universal-torsor obstruction and therefore vanishing higher Amitsur groups, while no weak-versality result was found. This packet closes it negatively by fixed K3 geometry.

The Fermat-discriminant No. 2.18 and non-Q8 Kummer subgroup remain candidates, but their complete cohomological and Condition-(A) audits are not both sealed.

## 8. Best candidate for a second genuinely new theorem?

The requested search produced two new theorems. The strongest single application is the smooth quartic double solid because all currently available Amitsur-type obstructions vanish. The infinite odd exceptional-conic-bundle family is the strongest structural application.

After those, the best unresolved target is the Fermat-discriminant No. 2.18 Fano threefold. It is the correct test case for a three-dimensional exceptional-network theorem.
