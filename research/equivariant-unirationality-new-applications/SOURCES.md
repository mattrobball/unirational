## B. Versality, Condition (A), and cohomological obstructions

1. Alexander Duncan and Zinovy Reichstein, **Versality of algebraic group actions and rational points on twisted varieties**, arXiv:1109.6093.  
   Weak, versal, and very versal actions; generic-torsor dictionary.

2. Alexander Duncan, **Equivariant unirationality of del Pezzo surfaces of degree 3 and 4**, arXiv:1410.8434; *European Journal of Mathematics* 2 (2016), 897–916.  
   Condition (A), positive results in degree at least 3, and the degree-2 Geiser-factor counterexample.

3. Federico Scavia, **A counterexample to the Sylow detection conjecture**, arXiv:2607.25118.  
   Current status of the `(C7:C3)×C2` degree-2 del Pezzo example and essential-dimension consequences.

4. Ivan Cheltsov, Yuri Tschinkel, and Zhijia Zhang, **Equivariant unirationality of Fano threefolds**, arXiv:2502.19598, current manuscript.  
   Current positive toolbox, index-at-least-two scope, and remaining Fano-threefold boundary.

5. Yuri Tschinkel and Zhijia Zhang, **Cohomological obstructions to equivariant unirationality**, arXiv:2504.10204; *International Mathematics Research Notices* (2026).  
   Ordinary and third Amitsur calculations for del Pezzo surfaces and Kummer quartic double solids.

6. Federico Scavia, Yuri Tschinkel, and Zhijia Zhang, **Cohomological invariants from the equivariant universal torsor**, arXiv:2605.02763.  
   Stable equivariant birational invariance of the universal-torsor class and the equivalence between its vanishing and simultaneous vanishing of all higher Amitsur groups in the stated geometric setting.

7. Hamid Abban, Ivan Cheltsov, Takashi Kishimoto, and Frédéric Mangolte, **Smooth Fano 3-folds satisfying Condition (A)**, arXiv:2505.13684.  
   Complete classification of smooth Fano threefold families satisfying Condition (A); every smooth member of Mori–Mukai family No. 1.10 passes.

8. Andrew Kresch and Yuri Tschinkel, **Equivariant Burnside groups: structure and operations**, arXiv:2105.02929, with companion Burnside papers.  
   Fixed-stratum birational invariants used to identify already-decided or partially covered cases.

## C. Smooth quartic surfaces and double solids

1. Jose Avila, Guillermo Ortiz, and Sergio Troncoso, **Invariant Smooth Quartic Surfaces by all Finite Primitive Groups of `PGL4(C)`**, arXiv:2211.13273.  
   Theorem A(4) gives the unique smooth quartic invariant under the primitive `PSL2(F7)` action:
   \[
   2x_0^4+6x_0x_1x_2x_3+x_1x_3^3+x_1^3x_2+x_2^3x_3=0.
   \]

2. Yuri Prokhorov, **A simple proof of the non-rationality of a general quartic double solid**, arXiv:1605.02017.  
   Introduction records the classical fact that smooth quartic double solids are unirational; only this ordinary unirationality statement is used here.

3. Ivan Cheltsov, **Kummer quartic double solids**, arXiv:2202.11668.  
   Automorphisms and equivariant birational rigidity of Kummer double solids.

4. Yuri Tschinkel and Zhijia Zhang, arXiv:2504.10204, above.  
   Third-Amitsur obstruction for selected Q8 actions on the special Kummer model.

## D. Conic-bundle surfaces and the plane Cremona group

1. Igor Dolgachev and Vasily Iskovskikh, **Finite subgroups of the plane Cremona group**, arXiv:math/0610595.  
   Exceptional conic-bundle model, automorphism structure, central de Jonquières involution, and fixed hyperelliptic curve.

2. Andrey Pinardin, **`G`-solid rational surfaces**, arXiv:2309.13757; *European Journal of Mathematics* 10 (2024).  
   Modern equivariant Sarkisov/solidity boundary for rational conic bundles.

3. Andrey Pinardin, Arman Sarikyan, and Egor Yasinsky, **Linearization problem for finite subgroups of the plane Cremona group**, arXiv:2412.12022, current version.  
   Projective-linearizability classification; used to distinguish nonlinearizability from non-`G`-unirationality.

## E. Rational Fano conic-bundle threefolds

1. Shuto Abe, **Automorphism groups and linearizability of rational Fano conic bundle threefolds**, arXiv:2506.15042, current revision.  
   Rationality of Mori–Mukai No. 2.18; double-cover, conic-bundle, and quadric-bundle models; automorphism formulas; Fermat and Klein discriminant examples; equivariant intermediate-Jacobian torsor obstruction to projective linearizability.

2. Tudor Ciurca, Sho Tanimoto, and Yuri Tschinkel, **Intermediate Jacobians and linearizability**, cited and generalized by Abe.  
   Equivariant IJ-torsor obstruction; it controls linearizability rather than domination by a linear source.

3. Ivan Cheltsov, Yuri Tschinkel, and Zhijia Zhang, arXiv:2502.19598, above.  
   General equivariant-unirationality context for Fano threefolds.

## F. Index-one Fano threefolds

1. Ivan Cheltsov and Constantin Shramov, **Three embeddings of the Klein simple group into the Cremona group of rank three**, arXiv:1010.1918.  
   Constructs
   \[
   X_{22}=\operatorname{VSP}(C_{\rm Klein},6),
   \]
   proves it is a smooth rational genus-12 Fano threefold with faithful `PSL2(F7)` action, records the anticanonical representation, and proves `G`-birational superrigidity.

2. Hamid Abban, Ivan Cheltsov, Takashi Kishimoto, and Frédéric Mangolte, arXiv:2505.13684, above.  
   Proves Condition (A) for every smooth member of family No. 1.10, including `X22`.

3. Federico Scavia, Yuri Tschinkel, and Zhijia Zhang, arXiv:2605.02763, above.  
   Since `Pic(X22)=Z[-K]` and `-K` is canonically `G`-linearized, the equivariant universal-torsor and all higher-Amitsur obstructions vanish.

4. Alexander Kuznetsov and Yuri Prokhorov, **Prime Fano threefolds of genus 12 with a `G_m`-action**, arXiv:1711.08504.  
   Explicit genus-12 models and automorphism classifications, especially for the Mukai–Umemura backup target.

5. Mikio Furushima, **Mukai–Umemura's example of the Fano threefold with genus 12 as a compactification of `C^3`**, *Nagoya Mathematical Journal* 127 (1992), 145–165.  
   Rationality of the Mukai–Umemura threefold.

6. The repository's `v14_not_weakly_versal.tex`.  
   First sealed index-one centralizer application and comparison target.

## G. Del Pezzo and singular-Fano classification boundaries

1. Ivan Cheltsov, Yuri Tschinkel, and Zhijia Zhang, **Birational geometry of actions on del Pezzo surfaces**, arXiv:2604.20425.  
   Current birational classification of regular generically free finite-group actions on del Pezzo surfaces.

2. Ivan Cheltsov, Ilya Krylov, and Sione Ma'u, **`G`-birationally rigid cubic threefolds**, arXiv:2604.20426.  
   Current cubic-threefold rigidity boundary; not used as an equivariant-unirationality theorem.

3. Ivan Cheltsov, Yuri Tschinkel, and Zhijia Zhang, **Equivariant geometry of the Segre cubic and the Burkhardt quartic**, arXiv:2308.15271, current revision.  
   Rational singular-Fano actions and linearizability/Burnside boundaries.

4. Prokhorov, Shramov, Trepalin, Blanc, Yasinsky, Avilov, and collaborators, classification papers cited in the works above.  
   Used to locate explicit Mori-fiber actions. No rigidity, solidity, or nonlinearizability statement was treated as an equivariant-unirationality theorem unless it explicitly addressed versality or domination by a linear source.
