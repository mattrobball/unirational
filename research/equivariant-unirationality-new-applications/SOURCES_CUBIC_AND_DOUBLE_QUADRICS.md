# Sources: cubic-surface bundles and quadratic double solids

**Search cutoff:** 2026-08-09.

This file supplements `SOURCES.md` for the two added target pools.

## A. Cubic-surface bundles

1. János Kollár, **Unirationality of cubic hypersurfaces**, arXiv:math/0005146; *Journal of the Institute of Mathematics of Jussieu* 1 (2002), 467–476.
   
   Proves that a smooth cubic hypersurface of dimension at least two over an arbitrary field is unirational when it has a rational point. This is applied to the generic cubic-surface fiber after exhibiting three rational sections.

2. Asher Auel, Christian Böhning, and Alena Pirutka, **Stable rationality of quadric and cubic surface bundle fourfolds**, arXiv:1710.07270.
   
   Current stable-rationality boundary for cubic-surface bundles; in particular, studies bidegree-`(2,3)` hypersurfaces in `P2 x P3`. It does not address the exact finite-group actions constructed here.

3. Alena Pirutka, **Cubic surface bundles and the Brauer group**, arXiv:2312.00144; current journal version.
   
   Computes unramified/Brauer information for cubic-surface bundles over rational surfaces. This is adjacent nonequivariant obstruction theory, not a weak-versality result for the packet’s actions.

4. Federico Scavia, Yuri Tschinkel, and Zhijia Zhang, **Birational invariance of higher Amitsur groups**, arXiv:2605.02763.
   
   For smooth projective `G`-varieties with free finitely generated Picard group, vanishing of the equivariant universal-torsor obstruction implies vanishing of every higher Amitsur group. Applied to the cubic-bundle family after Grothendieck--Lefschetz and explicit linearization of `O(1,0)` and `O(0,1)`.

5. Alexander Duncan and Zinovy Reichstein, **Versality of algebraic group actions and rational points on twisted varieties**, arXiv:1109.6093.
   
   Supplies the weak/very versality and generic-torsor dictionary.

## B. Double covers branched over quadrics

1. Ivan Cheltsov, Yuri Tschinkel, and Zhijia Zhang, **Equivariant unirationality of Fano threefolds**, arXiv:2502.19598, revision dated March 22, 2026.
   
   Proves that a generically free action on a smooth quadric threefold is stably linearizable exactly when Condition (A) holds. Therefore a double cover of `P3` branched over a smooth quadric—equivalently a smooth quadric threefold—is not a new negative target after Condition (A) passes.

## C. Genuine double quadrics

1. Victor Przyjalkowski and Constantin Shramov, **Double quadrics with large automorphism groups**, arXiv:1604.00307; *Proceedings of the Steklov Institute of Mathematics* 294 (2016), 154–175.
   
   Classifies nodal Fano threefolds that are double covers of smooth quadric threefolds branched over quartic sections and admit finite simple nonabelian group actions; analyzes rationality and supplies the `A5`/`A6` target pool.

2. Hamid Abban, Ivan Cheltsov, Alexander Kasprzyk, Yuchen Liu, and Andrea Petracci, **On K-moduli of quartic threefolds**, arXiv:2210.14781.
   
   Places smooth double quadrics in the Picard-rank-one anticanonical-volume-four family together with quartic threefolds, represented as `X_{2,4} subset P(1^5,2)`.

3. Alexandra Kuznetsova, **Double covers of smooth quadric threefolds with Artin--Mumford obstructions to rationality**, arXiv:2304.09955.
   
   Classifies three nodal double-quadric families carrying Artin--Mumford obstructions. This makes singular large-group examples lower-priority for a new fixed-geometry theorem unless the cohomological obstruction is first shown silent.

4. Constantin Shramov, **Birational rigidity and Q-factoriality of a singular double cover of a quadric branched over a divisor of degree 4**, *Mathematical Notes* 84 (2008), 280–289.
   
   Birational-rigidity and factoriality boundary for nodal double quadrics; not an equivariant-unirationality result.
